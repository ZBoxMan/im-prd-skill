## Baseline (follow by default, high confidence)

No entry in this module meets the baseline threshold: of five products, one runs no ads, and for two the ad rules mostly live on the server with only copy obtained on the client; no cross-product consistent practice has direct evidence from three products, so everything has been placed in conventions or divergences. The ad label (ADS-01) and the ad explanation page (ADS-03) have direct evidence from only one product plus regulatory corroboration; explicit regulatory text does not count toward the convention threshold, so they are placed in divergences marked default-OK.

## Conventions (default-OK, mark as suggested)

- **[ADS-02] Ads are not inserted into the message stream of one-on-one chats and group chats**: Ads appear only in one-to-many or standalone ad slots (public channels, feed and story streams, discovery and directory pages, standalone ad card slots), and are not inserted as a message into the chat history of a contact chat or group chat. Ad slot list see ADS-06. Must define: the list of ad slots this requirement involves, whether encrypted chats and stranger message requests show no ads at all, whether private channels and public channels are served alike (see ADS-20). | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence)
- **[ADS-04] Dismissing and reporting are two separate entries**: Ads provide a dismiss (hide) entry, and dismissing and reporting are separate: dismissing affects only what the user sees, while reporting enters review. Whether dismissing is paid see ADS-13, cooldown after dismissing see ADS-11, not interested see ADS-12. Must define: the list and order of ad menu items ("Close ad" / "Report ad" / "About this ad"), the menu entry (long-press or More button), the immediate behavior after dismissing (collapse, leave a one-line undo prompt, or disappear directly), whether the ad is hidden immediately after a report is submitted, the prompt when reporting an already-reported ad, and whether dismissing and reporting sync to the account's other devices. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence)
- **[ADS-05] Reporting an ad**: See SAFE-09; ad reports reuse the general report form; the ad reason list may be configured separately, and this module only defines the local behavior after a report in ADS-04.

## Divergences (ask Box on must-ask; write default-OK per recommendation and list it)

- **[ADS-01] Ad label and advertiser visibility**: Option A: wherever an ad appears, in whichever slot, it carries an "Ad"-type label that appears together with the ad, cannot be hidden separately, and is visually distinguishable from regular content; the advertiser name is visible with the ad or one tap away / Option B: only the "About this ad" explanation page states that it is an ad and names the advertiser. Recommended: A. Rationale: users understand and can tell ads from regular content; the one known ad-running product labels as in A, and ad regulations in some launch regions require every ad to be identifiable and to name the advertiser (some regions also require naming the payer, per launch-region compliance requirements). How a chat list ad entry is distinguished from a regular chat see ADS-07. Must define: label copy ("Ad" or "Sponsored") and translations per locale, label position in each ad slot (see the design draft), whether a screen reader reads the label first, whether the advertiser name is shown directly or on the explanation page, character limits for title and body (those that vary by locale are configured per locale), and the number of links an ad carries. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-03] The "About this ad" explanation page**: Option A: every ad has an explanation entry one tap away (tap the label or a menu item), and the page states at least the advertiser, why the user sees this ad (which main targeting parameters were used), which data the ad does not use (such as private chats and call content), and offers an entry to "Ad settings" / Option B: no per-ad explanation page, only a single place in settings that explains the ad rules. Recommended: A. Rationale: user understanding and control; "which data is not used" has direct evidence from two products, and the entry and advertiser have direct evidence from one product; some launch regions require showing the main targeting parameters and how to change them. Must define: entry position and copy, the field list of the explanation page, whether payer and advertiser are shown separately when they differ, the granularity of targeting parameter display (country or region, language, channel or feed, interests), and the retention period for ad interaction data (a source-of-truth gap; write {TBC} into open items). | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-06] Ad slot form**: Option A: ads only in one-to-many scenarios (feed and story streams, public channels, discovery and directory pages), none in the chat list or chat page / Option B: a standalone ad card slot in the chat list (distinguishable from chat rows and not a chat), plus ads in one-to-many scenarios / Option C: ads appear in the chat list as sponsored chats (tappable, followable, handled by chat rules). Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative (there is already a chat list ad slot plan, corresponding to B; unconfirmed, from a prior review example; verify with Box or the original Figma draft before writing the card); when undecided, recommend A. Rationale: no known product places platform ads in the contact chat list, the only exception being chats sponsored by an agency; a product that turned the contact list into a feed was rejected by users and rolled back. B contradicts existing products' practice and is a monetization trade-off that must be stated in the delivery draft. | Level: Must-ask | framing: yes
- **[ADS-07] How a chat list ad entry is distinguished from a regular chat**: Option A: the ad entry is not a chat: it counts toward no unread count or app badge, triggers no push, cannot be replied to, does not enter chat search or message search, does not respond to chat operations such as pin, mute, archive, mark as read, or swipe-left delete, does not take part in chat ordering (position set by ADS-08), and is skipped in multi-select and "Mark all as read" / Option B: the ad is a sponsored chat that opens the advertiser's channel or service account, counts unread by chat rules, and can be pinned and archived. Recommended: A. Rationale: user understanding and data integrity; counting ads into unread and badge would pollute the user's sense of "someone is looking for me"; B holds only when ADS-06 chooses C. Badge count basis see NOTI-07, search zones see MISC-01. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-08] Position of the chat list ad slot**: Option A: fixed at the Nth position below pinned chats (the Nth position in the list when nothing is pinned), not squeezed into the pinned area / Option B: fixed at the very top of the list, above pinned chats / Option C: not on the first screen, appearing after scrolling to a certain position. Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative; when undecided, recommend A. Rationale: chats the user actively pinned should rank above ads. Pin limit and ordering see LIST-01, LIST-20. Must define: whether a late-arriving ad is inserted on first-screen load (inserting makes the list jump; recommended: if it arrives late, skip it this time). | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-09] Frequency counting basis**: Option A: by time: a daily impression cap plus a minimum interval between two impressions / Option B: by number of content items in between: insert one ad after every several regular items, at most one after all regular content / Option C: by scenario: A for the chat list slot, B for feeds and channels. Recommended: C. Rationale: a slot with a fixed position can only be frequency-capped by time, while for feeds counting by items better fits the reading rhythm; cap values are delivered by the server and adjustable, and the client only complies. Must define: counting dimension (per account or per device, whether multiple devices are merged), the counter reset time (calendar day in the account's time zone, see UX-36), and cap values (ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative). | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-10] Impression counting basis**: Option A: count once when the whole ad enters the viewport / Option B: count once only when more than half of the ad's visible area stays in view continuously for at least a set duration. Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative (the existing plan counts by dwell time; unconfirmed, from a prior review example; verify with Box or the original Figma draft before writing the card); when undecided, recommend B. Rationale: fast scroll-past does not count as an impression, so the frequency cap is not exhausted by ineffective impressions. Must define: whether scrolling back and forth within one browse counts repeatedly, whether timing pauses when the app goes to the background or the screen locks, whether impressions are cached and reported later when offline, and whether a click must first record an impression. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-11] Cooldown after dismissing**: Option A: dismiss a single ad: this ad no longer appears, and the ad slot shows no ads during the cooldown (hours to days), resuming when it ends / Option B: dismissing closes that ad slot long-term, to be restored manually in ad settings / Option C: hide only this ad, and the next ad appears on the next refresh. Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative (the existing plan sets a cooldown period; unconfirmed, from a prior review example; verify with Box or the original Figma draft before writing the card); when undecided, recommend A. Rationale: dismissing expresses "I don't want to see this now"; C makes dismissing meaningless, and B directly affects revenue and needs separate discussion. Must define: cooldown duration, whether the cooldown syncs across devices, whether other ad slots keep showing during the cooldown, and whether the toast copy states the cooldown duration. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-12] Not interested and reason feedback**: Option A: merged into dismissing: after dismissing, optional reasons appear (not interested, too repetitive, inappropriate content), skippable / Option B: "Not interested" as its own menu item that reduces similar ads, while dismissing only hides this ad / Option C: collect no reason. Recommended: A. Rationale: the fewest menu items, with one action expressing both hiding and the reason; when personalization is off, the reason affects only the same advertiser and the same category and is not used for profiling. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-13] Whether ad removal is a paid benefit**: Option A: dismissing a single ad is free, with no paid ad removal / Option B: dismissing a single ad is free, and a paid subscription removing all ads is also offered / Option C: dismissing ads itself requires payment, and free users can only report. Recommended: A. Rationale: user control; charging for the dismiss entry makes "dismiss" a mere decoration; paid ad removal is a monetization topic and no card is written when it is out of scope for this release; when B is chosen, subscription activation, renewal, cancellation and expiry follow module 24 (activation channel see PTS-23). | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-14] Default for personalization**: Option A: no personalization: serve only by current scenario (the channel or feed), country or region, and language, with no personalization toggle / Option B: personalization off by default, and users turn it on actively in ad settings / Option C: personalization on by default, and users can turn it off in settings. Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative (the current privacy policy says targeted ads are not run at present, corresponding to A); when personalization needs to be introduced, recommend B. Rationale: stricter privacy defaults; some launch regions require personalization to have separate consent or be easy to turn off. Whether personalized content recommendations (recommendation streams in feeds, channels and the discovery page) share the same default as personalized ads belongs to this topic as well. | Level: Must-ask
- **[ADS-15] Data scope for targeting (whether chat content is used)**: Option A: private chats, group chats, call content and the address book are never used for ads and recommendations; only scenario signals (country or region, language, the public channel or feed the user is in) and interactions with the ad itself are used / Option B: additionally use profile and public in-app behavior (followed channels, interactions with public posts) / Option C: additionally use content keywords from unencrypted chats. Recommended: A. Rationale: privacy and data integrity; the two known ad-running products both publicly commit to not using private chat and call content; C conflicts with the end-to-end encryption commitment. Sensitive-category data such as health, religion, political views and sexual orientation is not used for profiling under any option. | Level: Must-ask
- **[ADS-16] Form and entry of the personalization toggles**: Option A: under "Settings" - "Privacy and data", an "Ad settings" page with two toggles, "Personalized ads" and "Personalized content recommendations", reachable directly from the ad explanation page, with turning off lasting indefinitely and syncing across devices / Option B: a single master toggle / Option C: changeable only on the phone, with other devices showing only the status. Recommended: A. Rationale: users can understand and reverse it; ads and content recommendations are governed by different rules, and a merged toggle makes turning one off carry the other with it. Must define: the entry path, the toggle explanation copy, the note that turning it off does not change the number of ads, only reduces relevance, and that turning off has no expiry (setting an expiry is an anti-pattern); data already used for personalization after turning off see PRIV-13. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-17] Ad handling for underage accounts**: Option A: accounts determined to be underage see no personalized ads or personalized recommendations, only non-personalized ads, with age-inappropriate ad categories excluded / Option B: accounts determined to be underage see no ads at all / Option C: handled the same as adult accounts. Recommended: A. Rationale: regulations in some launch regions prohibit profiling-based ads or automated-decision marketing toward minors; B is stricter but affects revenue, and if ZYMIX's minimum registration age is adult, this mainly applies to accounts that have not completed age confirmation. Age determination basis and which tier accounts that have not completed age confirmation fall under see AGE-07; the list of age-inappropriate categories is a source-of-truth gap; write {TBC} into open items. | Level: Must-ask
- **[ADS-18] Where tapping an ad goes, and external links**: Option A: prefer in-app landing (channel, service account, mini program or in-app browser); external links open in the in-app browser with the domain shown, without directly launching external apps / Option B: allow jumping directly to an external browser or application store. Recommended: A. Rationale: recoverable (back returns to the original position) and allows risky external link prompts, reducing third-party tracking. Risky external link prompt see SAFE-14. Must define: the prompt and destination when the landing page is invalid (channel dissolved, service account disabled, link expired). | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-19] Insertion and interaction of feed ads**: Option A: the first item on the first screen is not an ad, and ads are inserted afterward at the ADS-09 interval; ads offer no like or comment, only dismiss, report and "About this ad" / Option B: ads can be liked and commented on, with interactions visible only to mutual contacts. Recommended: A. Rationale: user understanding; contacts' interactions under an ad would turn the ad into social content and expose contacts' behavior; B needs a separate interaction visibility scope, to be discussed after the interaction visibility rules in FEED-21. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ADS-20] Channel ad serving conditions and channel owner control**: Option A: serve only in public channels whose subscriber count reaches a threshold (thousands level), at the end of the post stream or inserted at the ADS-09 interval; a channel owner meeting certain conditions can turn off ads for their channel, and does not take part in ad revenue sharing after turning off / Option B: the platform serves ads uniformly in all public channels, and channel owners cannot turn them off / Option C: no ads in channels. Recommended: A. Rationale: private channels and small channels are not served, reducing disturbance to small circles and giving channel owners control; when revenue sharing is out of scope for this release, write only the serving conditions. Channel public status and discoverability see FEED-12. | Level: Default-OK

## Must-ask states (module-specific, supplements the general state matrix)
- No ad to serve: when fetching fails, the ad has expired, inventory is empty, or a cooldown or frequency cap is hit, whether the ad slot collapses without a placeholder or keeps a placeholder; whether an ad that arrives late on first-screen load is inserted.
- Account state: what each ad slot shows for accounts with personalization off, underage, age confirmation not completed, in a region with no ad serving, or with paid ad removal.
- Multi-device: when dismissing, cooldown, reporting, not interested and the personalization toggles sync to the account's other devices; whether desktop and tablet show ad slots.
- Coexisting with chat operations: whether the ad entry appears and how it responds during swipe-left, long-press, multi-select, mark all as read, chat search, archive view, chat groups, and mute filter.
- Impressions and timing: how impressions are counted across background, screen lock, interruption by an incoming call, fast scroll-past and pull-to-refresh; whether impressions and clicks are cached and reported later when offline.
- Action in flight: when dismissing or reporting fails on the network, whether it takes effect locally first; prompt and retry for a failed report submission; switching accounts during a cooldown.
- Landing invalid: at tap time the ad is offline, the landing channel is dissolved, the service account is disabled, or the external link is expired.
- Language and region: ad language and label copy after switching UI language; title character limits and truncation per locale; which region to serve by when roaming across regions.
- Encrypted and private scenarios: whether encrypted chats, stranger message requests, private channels and groups show no ads at all.

## Anti-patterns
| Vague/wrong | Correct | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "Show an ad at the Nth position of the chat list" | Specify the label, how it differs from a chat (unread, badge, push, search, chat operations), and no placeholder when there is no ad | The ad is handled as a chat, polluting the unread and badge basis |
| Only "ads can be dismissed" | Specify the cooldown after dismissing, cross-device sync, dismiss and report being separate, and where not interested goes | Engineering builds only local hiding, and the ad reappears on the next refresh |
| Frequency written only as "at most N times per day" | Specify the daily cap, minimum interval, counting dimension (account or device), reset time, and impression basis | With different bases, actual impressions under the same cap can differ several times over |
| The personalization toggle is buried in multi-level menus, or expires periodically after being turned off | A single entry at "Settings" - "Privacy and data", reachable directly from the ad explanation page, with turning off lasting indefinitely | A hidden entry and periodic expiry get called out by regulators |
| "About this ad" says only "Learn more" | Specify the advertiser, why the user sees it, which data is not used, and the entry to ad settings | Launch regions require showing the main targeting parameters and how to change them |
| Targeting data written as "based on your interests" | List the data used and not used, and state that private chats, call content and the address book are not used for ads | Users cannot tell whether chat content is being read |
| Underage accounts are served the same as adult accounts | Underage accounts get no personalization, age-inappropriate categories are excluded, and the age determination basis references the same definition | Profiling ads toward minors are prohibited in many places |
| The ad label is done in Chinese only | The label and the explanation page are fully translated per locale, and the title limit is configured per locale | In minor languages, a missing label means the ad is not labeled |

## Calibration sample
The feature most prone to vague writing: the chat list ad slot. Writing only "there is an ad in the list" misses the label, how it differs from chats, frequency and cooldown, dismissing and reporting, and personalization and underage handling. The ad slot form was already settled at framing as a standalone ad card slot in the chat list; the distinguishing rules, position, frequency, impression basis, cooldown and dismiss method are written into the body per the recommendations, with values in the card illustrative; the personalization default is a privacy default, but the recommendation says ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative, and the current privacy policy says targeted ads are not run, so it is written into the body per the source of truth and not asked; the targeting data scope and underage account handling are privacy defaults and must-ask, so the card leaves placeholders; the list of age-inappropriate ad categories is written as {TBC}. Display conditions are not pre-checks after a tap, so they are written as steps and no decision chain is used.

Description:
1. Scope: the chat list ad card slot, at the 1st position below pinned chats; the 1st position in the list when there are no pinned chats
2. Default: one ad is shown only when all of the following hold: not within a dismiss cooldown, fewer than 3 impressions today, at least 2 hours since the last impression, and the server returns a servable ad; impressions are counted per account, merged across devices, and reset on the calendar day in the account's time zone
3. If any condition is not met, or the ad fetch fails or the ad has expired: the slot takes no space, the list shows regular chats as normal, and no blank or loading state is shown; if the ad returns only after the first screen has loaded, it is not inserted this time
4. Label: the ad card always carries an "Ad" badge that cannot be turned off; a screen reader reads "Ad" first, then the advertiser name and title
5. Distinction from chats: the ad card counts toward no unread count or app badge, triggers no push, and cannot be replied to, pinned, muted or archived, and does not appear in search results; multi-select and "Mark all as read" skip the ad card
6. Impression basis: an impression is counted once when more than half of the ad card is in the viewport and stays continuously for at least 1 second; scrolling back and forth within one browse is not counted again; timing stops when the app goes to the background or the screen locks
7. Tapping the ad card: opens an in-app landing page (channel, service account or in-app browser); external links open in the in-app browser with the domain shown; when the landing page is invalid, toast: "This content is no longer available", and stay on the chat list
8. Long-pressing the ad card or tapping the More button opens a menu: "Close ad" / "Report ad" / "About this ad"
9. Tapping "Close ad": the ad card collapses immediately, toast: "Closed. No ads for 24 hours"; then optional reasons "Not interested" / "Too repetitive" / "Inappropriate content" appear, skippable; the cooldown takes effect in sync on the account's other devices; on a network failure it collapses locally first and is reported after reconnecting
10. Tapping "Report ad": opens report reason selection, with the same flow as message reports; after a successful submission the ad collapses immediately, toast: "Report received. We will handle it as soon as possible"; with no network, toast: "No network connection. Check your network and try again", and on a submission failure, toast: "Network error. Please try again", and in both cases the ad card stays as it was
11. Tapping "About this ad": shows the advertiser name, why the user sees this ad, and the data this ad does not use, with an "Ad settings" entry at the bottom
12. Personalization: no personalization, serving only by country or region, language and current scenario, per the current privacy policy

Underage accounts: {TBD: ad handling for underage accounts, recommended: show no personalized ads and exclude age-inappropriate ad categories}; age-inappropriate categories {TBC: list of age-inappropriate ad categories for launch regions}; which accounts are handled as underage (including accounts that have not completed age confirmation) is consistent with the determination rules of the age confirmation feature
