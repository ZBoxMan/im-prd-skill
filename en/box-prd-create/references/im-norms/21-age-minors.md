# Age assurance and minors (minimum age, age confirmation, defaults for unconfirmed accounts, minor mode, guardians)

> Applies: read this file when a PRD involves the age threshold at registration, the birthday field, age confirmation (age estimation, documents, identity verification), feature restrictions for accounts that have not completed age confirmation, age-restricted content, handling of underage accounts once found, minor mode and guardians. Legal thresholds and clause details are not written into this file or annotation cards; they are uniformly written as "per launch-region compliance requirements, {TBC}"; for legal values see evidence/21-age-minors.md.

## Baseline (follow by default, high confidence)
- **[AGE-01] Write the minimum age into the terms and take the highest by region**: the terms state the minimum age at which a user may register and use the product on their own; when a launch region's legal threshold is higher, take the higher one; users below the threshold cannot register and use it on their own. Must define: the minimum age value (see AGE-06); the legal threshold of each launch region (per launch-region compliance requirements, {TBC}); at which step of the registration flow age is declared or collected; the threshold is consistent across the terms, the registration page and the age confirmation page. | Magnitude: 13 to 18 years | Confidence: High
- **[AGE-02] Age-restricted spots state the restriction and give a way to lift it**: when an age-restricted feature or content is accessed, the restriction is stated at the entry or the content, and the currently available path to lift it is given (go confirm age, or request guardian authorization). Must define: for each item, whether the entry is hidden, grayed out, or tappable with a prompt (money-related entries may be hidden outright); the prompt copy; where the lifting path leads (confirmation methods in AGE-09, guardian approval in AGE-05); the copy and destination when age has been confirmed below the threshold and there is no lifting path. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: High

## Conventions (default-OK, mark as suggested)
- **[AGE-03] State the reason and basis before requiring confirmation**: when asking the user to confirm age or identity, the confirmation page states why it is needed, including a basis such as "as required by regulations in your region". Must define: whether the basis copy switches by region (for reference: regions where regulations require confirmation state the basis, other regions do not); the confirmation methods and required materials; how materials are handled and what is retained (see AGE-10); the consequence of not confirming (see AGE-07). | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium
- **[AGE-04] Guardian relationships are confirmed by both sides**: applies only to products that provide guardians; a guardian relationship must be confirmed by both the ward and the guardian (accepting after an invitation, or pairing two devices), and cannot be established by one side acting alone. Must define: who initiates; the invitation validity period (for reference: valid for the same day); the prompts each side sees on establishing and ending; whether the ward can remove or block the guardian (for reference: cannot be removed, the guardianship must be ended first); how many guardians one account can have; handling when the guardian deletes their account or is penalized. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium
- **[AGE-05] Restricted features are unlocked by the guardian**: applies only to products that provide guardians; features restricted under minor mode are unlocked by the guardian, and the ward cannot lift the restriction themselves. Must define: the unlock method (approve after a per-use request, or the guardian changes settings directly, see AGE-08); the request status chain (requested, approved, rejected, expired) and how it is presented in the chat; the unlock validity period (for reference: valid for the same day); what the ward and the guardian each see after it expires. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium

## Divergences (ask Box on must-ask; write default-OK per recommendation and list it)
- **[AGE-06] Minimum age and whether to serve minors**: Option A: the minimum age is set to adult, no minor mode is provided, and accounts found below the threshold are handled per AGE-11 / Option B: the minimum age takes the legal lower bound of the launch region (mostly 13 to 16), underage accounts enter minor mode and can bind a guardian / Option C: the minimum age takes the legal lower bound, no guardians, and defaults are only tightened by age. Recommended: A, per existing ZYMIX terms (cite old terms only with a date). Rationale: student social networking involves stranger contact and public posting, and serving minors requires a full set of guardianship and tightening capabilities; two products are known to use 13 as the self-use lower bound, and one sets 18 in some regions. The legal lower bound of each launch region is per launch-region compliance requirements, {TBC}. | Level: Must-ask | Framing: yes
- **[AGE-07] Age determination basis and the default for accounts that have not completed age confirmation**: determination basis: the age confirmation result is authoritative, and a self-entered birthday is only profile data and does not count as confirmation (methods in AGE-09). Option A: age confirmation must be completed at registration, and the user cannot enter without it / Option B: registration and use are allowed, and before confirmation is completed the account is tightened to minor defaults, with in-place guidance to confirm when a restricted feature is used (see the lifting path in AGE-02) / Option C: treated as adult, and confirmation is required only before features where regulations require it. The tightening dimensions include at least: stranger contact (model in REL-21), default audience for feed posts and stories (see FEED-20), payment and wallet account opening (see PAY-23), age-restricted content (see AGE-14), whether strangers can find the account in search, ads and personalized recommendation (see ADS-17), lotteries, redemption and enabling membership. Recommended: B. Rationale: privacy and compliance risk first, without blocking registration; among five products only one has direct evidence of restrictions on unconfirmed accounts (a sensitive-content mask, not limited to social), and B is stricter than existing products, which is a compliance trade-off. Consequence note: when production new accounts have no birthday filled in and no age restrictions, choosing B puts almost all new and existing accounts into the tightened state, so an age confirmation entry and existing-account migration must be provided at the same time, and the question text must say so. The age confirmation methods recognized by launch regions are a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC} into open items. | Level: Must-ask
- **[AGE-08] Scope of guardian capabilities**: applies only when AGE-06 chooses to serve minors. Option A: request-and-approve: the ward requests temporary use of restricted features each time, and the guardian approves to unlock; the guardian can also set usage hours and a spending cap; the guardian sees only usage duration and request records, and cannot see chat partners or content / Option B: guardian sets directly: the guardian manages the ward's contact scope, group joining and privacy settings on their own device, settings are locked behind guardian verification, and stranger contact requests are reviewed by the guardian / Option C: B plus risk-behavior notifications, notifying the guardian when suspected social risk is identified. Recommended: A. Rationale: direct evidence from two products adopts request-and-approve, so unlocks are traceable; the guardian cannot see chat partners or content, which minimizes intrusion into the ward's privacy. | Level: Must-ask
- **[AGE-09] Age confirmation method**: Option A: self-declaration (tick "meets the threshold" or enter a birthday) / Option B: facial age estimation, moving to document verification when the result is close to the threshold or fails / Option C: document or licensed-institution identity verification / Option D: other third-party age proof (carrier, bank card, digital identity). Recommended: B, with C as the fallback, per the existing ZYMIX privacy policy. Rationale: self-declaration is not recognized in some regions, and B has the least friction and may retain no raw image. Which methods launch regions recognize and the buffer range near the threshold are source-of-truth gaps, write {TBC} into open items. | Level: Default-OK
- **[AGE-10] Retention and visibility of age confirmation data**: Option A: keep only the result (whether it meets the threshold, or the age bracket) and the confirmation time, delete raw images and documents right after confirmation; the result is not visible to other users, and syncs across the user's own devices / Option B: keep raw materials for a period for review and appeals / Option C: show a confirmed badge or age bracket on the profile page to others. Recommended: A, per the existing ZYMIX privacy policy. Rationale: data minimization; the consequences of leaked verification materials far outweigh the benefit of retention. | Level: Must-ask
- **[AGE-11] Finding an account below the minimum age**: triggers (report, age confirmation result, birthday changed to below the threshold) all belong to this entry. Option A: close the account, delete personal data within a period, with an appeal (re-confirming age) possible before deletion / Option B: freeze the account, restoring it after guardian consent or re-confirmation / Option C: move to minor mode (only when AGE-06 chooses to serve minors). The wallet balance and unfinished amounts in the account: see WAL-18. Recommended: A, per existing ZYMIX terms. Rationale: when the threshold is adult there is no mode to move into; an appeal leaves a way out for misjudgments. | Level: Must-ask
- **[AGE-12] Birthday changes and crossing the threshold**: Option A: the birthday can be changed with a limited number of changes, and when a change crosses the threshold age confirmation must be completed again, with the account treated as unconfirmed until then / Option B: after age confirmation is completed the birthday is locked and can be changed only through support or re-confirmation / Option C: unlimited changes. Recommended: A. Rationale: keeps room for corrections while closing the loophole of changing the birthday to bypass the threshold; the count follows existing ZYMIX rules. | Level: Default-OK
- **[AGE-13] Visibility scope of the birthday in the profile**: Option A: visible only to oneself by default, can be changed to contacts or everyone, and the year can be hidden separately / Option B: visible to contacts by default, the year can be hidden / Option C: the birthday is not shown in the profile and is used only for age determination. Whether to list the birthday as a privacy setting is in REL-25. Recommended: A. Rationale: the birthday is key data for inferring age and verifying identity, so the default is minimum exposure. | Level: Must-ask
- **[AGE-14] Display of age-restricted content**: applies only to products that have adult or age-restricted content. Option A: unconfirmed accounts see a mask, and tapping guides them to confirm; after confirmation meets the threshold it can be viewed and set to always show; accounts confirmed below the threshold cannot open it / Option B: not shown at all to unconfirmed and below-threshold accounts, appearing in neither lists nor search / Option C: no age distinction, relying only on content moderation. Recommended: A. Rationale: the user knows the content exists and knows how to lift the restriction (see the lifting path in AGE-02); illegal or violating content is blocked entirely for everyone and is not covered by this entry. | Level: Default-OK
- **[AGE-15] In which regions age confirmation is triggered**: Option A: required uniformly in all launch regions / Option B: required only in regions where regulations require it, with other regions keeping self-declaration. Recommended: A. Rationale: a single rule has fewer states. The basis for determining the user's region (registration phone number, store region or network location) is a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC} into open items. | Level: Default-OK
- **[AGE-16] Usage hours and duration in minor mode**: applies only when AGE-06 chooses to serve minors. Option A: a nighttime lockout period plus a daily duration cap, adjustable by the guardian / Option B: only duration reminders, no forced lockout / Option C: none. Recommended: B. Rationale: known nighttime lockout start and end times differ by product and are mostly driven by regional regulations; whether a forced lockout is required is per launch-region compliance requirements, {TBC}, and once found, write per the requirement instead. | Level: Default-OK
- **[AGE-17] Default feed audience for minor or unconfirmed accounts**: See FEED-20. Scope: audience tiers and how to tighten belong to FEED-20; which accounts count as minor or unconfirmed follows AGE-07.
- **[AGE-18] Age threshold for payment and wallet account opening**: See PAY-23. Scope: the threshold and verification method belong to PAY-23; the default for unconfirmed accounts follows AGE-07.
- **[AGE-19] Stranger contact model**: See REL-21. Scope: the contact model belongs to REL-21; whether unconfirmed or minor accounts are tightened further on that basis follows AGE-07.
- **[AGE-20] Ads and personalized recommendation for minor or unconfirmed accounts**: See ADS-17. Scope: whether personalized ads and personalized content are shown to them and which ad categories are excluded belong to ADS-17; which accounts count as minor or unconfirmed follows AGE-07.

## Must-ask states (module-specific, supplementing the general state matrix)
- Existing accounts: accounts registered before the rule goes live with an empty or unconfirmed birthday: their state on launch day, the timing of the first prompt, and the grace period.
- Confirmation process: exiting midway, camera permission denied (see DISC-02), network interruption, estimation failure, moving to documents when the result is close to the threshold, cooldown after repeated failures, confirmation service unavailable.
- Result across devices: when restrictions lift on other devices after one device confirms; the state on each device after a confirmation result is revoked on review.
- Birthday changes: from below the threshold to meeting it, from meeting it to below the threshold, change count used up.
- In-progress content: when an account enters the tightened state or is determined to be below the threshold, how existing stranger chats, already-public feed posts, pending receipts, and joined age-restricted groups or channels are handled.
- The other party's view: what the party interacting with an unconfirmed or minor account sees (DMs blocked, not findable, cannot see feed posts), and whether it can be distinguished from blocks caused by the other party's privacy settings.
- Crossing the threshold: when a ward reaches the threshold age, whether restrictions and the guardian relationship end automatically or need confirmation, and what the guardian sees at that time.
- Guardian relationship: invitation expired, guardian deleted their account or is penalized, ward removed or blocked the guardian, guardian changed, either side changes device.
- Region changes: changing the phone number's region, and which region's threshold and confirmation requirements apply when used across regions.
- During underage handling: signing in while the account is closed pending deletion, during an appeal, and whether data is fully restored after a successful appeal.

## Anti-patterns
| Vague / wrong | Correct | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| The registration page has only a birthday input box, treated as age assurance | The birthday is only profile data; restricted capabilities follow the age confirmation result, and the card states the confirmation method and the default for unconfirmed accounts | A self-entered birthday can be filled in at will, and most regions do not recognize self-declaration |
| Accounts that have not completed age confirmation get all features by default | State the tightening list for unconfirmed accounts and the in-place confirmation entry | When a new account's birthday is empty, it is the same as having no threshold |
| Restricted features written only as "unavailable" | State per item whether the entry is hidden, grayed out or prompts on tap, and the lifting path | The user does not know why they cannot use it or how to lift the restriction |
| Age confirmation failure written only as "Verification failed" | Write separate copy and destinations for network failure, unable to determine, result below the threshold, and too many attempts | Estimation has an uncertainty range, and the failure cause decides the next step |
| Statute numbers, regulator names and clause details in the card | Write "as required by regulations in your region", and write regional thresholds and basis as {TBC} into open items | Regulations change, and the card states only product rules |
| Citing the minimum age without marking the basis date | State the threshold of the current terms, and mark old material with its date | The threshold has changed before, and old values would leak into new cards |
| Writing only the new registration flow | Also write migration of existing accounts, the first prompt and the grace period | Existing accounts are the most numerous when a rule goes live |
| Keeping estimation photos or documents long-term | Keep only the result and confirmation time, and delete raw materials after confirmation | The consequences of leaked verification materials far outweigh the benefit of retention |

## Calibration sample
The feature most likely to be written vaguely: age confirmation (in-place guidance and the confirmation page). After the tap there are three preconditions, cooldown, network and camera permission, so a decision chain is used; the confirmation methods, failure copy and cooldown count are written as body text per the recommendation, and values in the card are illustrative; the methods recognized by launch regions are written as {TBC}; the handling of a confirmation result below the minimum age and the retention of confirmation materials are irreversible disposal and privacy and would normally be must-ask, but the recommendation states it follows existing ZYMIX terms and the privacy policy, and when the source of truth already has the answers, they are written as body text per the source of truth and are not asked again.

Description:
1. Entry: when an account that has not completed age confirmation taps a restricted feature, a "Age confirmation needed" prompt appears, or the user enters from "Settings" - "Account" - "Age confirmation"; the confirmation page states the reason confirmation is needed, the confirmation methods and how materials are handled, and after ticking "I have read and agree to the age confirmation notice" the "Start confirmation" button lights up
2. On tap, check in order:
   a. 1-Cooldown check: whether this account is within the cooldown after too many failed confirmations
      i. In cooldown: dialog prompt: "Too many attempts. Try again in {remaining time}", button "Got it", after closing stay on the confirmation page
      i. Not in cooldown: go to the next check
   a. 2-Network check: whether there is a network now
      i. No network: toast prompt: "Network unavailable. Check your network and try again", stay on the confirmation page
      i. Network available: go to the next check
   a. 3-Camera permission check: whether the camera is authorized
      i. Authorized: go to facial age estimation
      i. Never requested and allowed in the system authorization dialog: go to facial age estimation
      i. Never requested and denied in the system authorization dialog: stay on the confirmation page, inline prompt: "Camera permission is needed for facial age estimation", button "Use a document instead"
      i. Previously denied: dialog prompt: "Camera permission is needed for facial age estimation. You can turn it on in system settings", buttons "Go to Settings" / "Use a document instead"; tap "Go to Settings" to jump to this app's permission page in system settings
3. Estimation result meets the threshold: toast prompt: "Age confirmation completed", return to the page before confirmation and lift the restriction; other signed-in devices of this account lift the restriction in sync
4. Estimation result is close to the threshold or cannot be determined: inline prompt: "We can't confirm your age right now. Please use a document instead", buttons "Use a document instead" / "Later"; choosing "Later" returns to the source page, and the account stays unconfirmed
5. Estimation fails (no face detected, insufficient light, exited midway): inline prompt: "Couldn't complete confirmation. Please try again", button "Retry"; ≥ 5 failures within 24 hours: age confirmation is paused for 24 hours, dialog prompt: "Too many attempts. Try again in 24 hours", button "Got it", after closing return to the source page
6. Confirmation result is below the minimum age: dialog prompt: "Based on the confirmation result, you do not meet the minimum age requirement for this service. Your account will be closed and your personal data will be deleted within 30 days", button "Got it"; the account is closed immediately, and personal data is deleted within 30 days, per the current terms
7. The images and documents used for confirmation are deleted as soon as confirmation completes, keeping only the age bracket and confirmation time, per the current privacy policy; the confirmation result is not shown to others on the profile page
8. The available confirmation methods follow regional regulatory requirements, {TBC: age confirmation methods and thresholds recognized by launch regions}
Note: the available methods and thresholds are to be replaced after the compliance reply
