# In-chat payments: transfers, red packets, payment requests and bill splitting

> Applies to: read this file when a PRD involves in-chat transfers, red packets or cash gifts, payment requests and group payment requests, bill splitting, payment verification, payment limits, and regional and real-name compliance. Regulated fund handling for the wallet is usually performed by a licensed institution. Whenever you write about fund states, first distinguish "regulated fund flow" (executed and confirmed by the licensed institution) from "bookkeeping" (ZYMIX's internal ledger, which only records and does not move money); see the first item under "Must-ask states". For evidence see evidence/14-wallet-payments.md.

## Baseline (follow by default, high confidence)
- **[PAY-01] The initiator cannot unilaterally cancel after a payment succeeds**: once a transfer, red packet or payment succeeds, the initiator cannot cancel it themselves; recovery can only come through the other party returning it, a timeout return, or support assistance. Must define: whether unsending the payment card in the chat affects the funds (when it does not, whether the unsend entry is hidden in the long-press menu); whether there are exception forms (e.g. a face-to-face-type red packet not yet claimed can be taken back early, within the scope of fund returns, treat as must-ask); the explanation of recovery routes the initiator can see on the card (following PAY-18 and PAY-20). | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: High
- **[PAY-02] Failure, cancellation and return state where the money went**: when a payment fails, is canceled, is returned or is returned on timeout, both parties can see where the money is: not debited, returned to where, deposited into where. Must define: the fund-destination copy for each final state (one sentence each for not debited, returned, credited); the arrival time of a return (balance instant, bank card by business day); the return destination itself see PAY-21. | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: High
- **[PAY-03] No promise to recover completed payments**: once the payment has been accepted by the other party, the platform does not promise to recover it; the explanation only says "contact the other party to return it" or "contact support for assistance", and disputes are handled by the payment institution and support per the rules. Must define: where the explanation copy appears (transfer page, card details, help page); the support or dispute entry; the boundary of what support can do (help contact the other party, no guarantee of recovery). | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: High
- **[PAY-04] Payment capability opens by region**: payment features open by region, and users in unavailable regions do not see them or cannot use them. Must define: the determination basis (phone country code, account region, identity verification nationality) and the list of open regions (depends on the licensed institution and launch-region regulation, a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC} into open items, do not ask Box); whether the entry is hidden or shows a prompt on tap when unavailable, and the prompt copy; what each party sees when one party is available and the other is not; how the balance is transferred out after a region goes offline (treat as must-ask). | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: High
- **[PAY-05] Provide personal verification before fund actions**: before paying, transferring, sending a red packet or withdrawing, provide a personal verification independent of the chat login (one of payment password, PIN, biometrics, two-step verification password or confirmation on the phone); whether every transaction must be verified and whether it can be skipped are not part of this entry. Must define: the verification factor and whether it can be skipped (see PAY-29); the cap on consecutive failures, lock duration and prompt (wording see SAFE-13); the verification method on desktop (verify on this client or hand off to phone confirmation); the entry to recover a forgotten payment password; preconditions the client can judge, such as balance, go before personal verification (insufficient balance gives "Top up" and "Change amount"), so users do not learn only after verifying that they cannot pay. | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: High

## Conventions (default-OK, mark as suggested)
- **[PAY-06] A transfer has processing intermediate states, with both parties' views written separately**: between payment submission and the final state there is a "Processing" or "Awaiting collection" intermediate state, and the initiator's and recipient's card copy are written separately (such as "Waiting for {nickname} to collect" and "Waiting for you to collect"). Must define: the intermediate state list (whether processing, awaiting collection, and collected pending credit each exist); both parties' card copy for each state; whether the user can leave the page while processing and where the result can be seen afterward; the final state list (collected, returned, expired and returned, failed); the idempotency basis (a payment is accepted only once per client unique request ID, a claim is deduplicated by account plus red packet); the maximum confirmation time for processing and the final-state copy after timeout. | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: Medium (intermediate states have direct evidence from two products; dual-view copy has direct evidence from two products)
- **[PAY-07] Operation restrictions on payment messages**: transfer, red packet and payment request cards cannot be forwarded; handling when mixed in multi-select see ACT-03. Must define: whether they can be saved to favorites and whether they can be viewed after saving; whether they can be quoted or reacted to and what is shown when quoted (e.g. "Transfer: {amount}"); whether they can be edited; whether unsending the card affects the funds (see PAY-01); whether the chat list summary and notification preview show the amount. | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: Medium (direct evidence from two products; a merchant invoice being forwardable at one product is a counterexample that does not apply to person-to-person payments)
- **[PAY-08] Block when the other party cannot collect, and give the next step**: when the other party has not opened a wallet, has not completed verification or has an invalid collection account, block before debiting and state the reason, giving a next step (e.g. "Remind them to open one"), never failing silently and never debiting first and refunding later. Must define: the block moment (when picking the recipient or after tapping pay); the copy for each reason (whether a restricted or frozen counterpart wallet is a separate reason see WAL-22); whether "Remind them to open one" generates a system message in the chat and its copy and frequency limit; whether the initiator is notified after the other party opens one. | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: Medium (direct evidence from two products; one product silently does not deliver to those who have not passed friend verification as a counterexample)
- **[PAY-09] No sending or collecting with parties without an established chat relationship**: when the other party has not accepted a message request or passed friend verification, you cannot transfer or send a red packet to them. Whether strangers can message directly see REL-21. Must define: whether the entry is hidden or prompts on tap; the prompt copy; whether sending a red packet or transfer to a non-friend member in a group is allowed. | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: Medium (direct evidence from two products)
- **[PAY-10] Unsupported clients and old-version downgrade**: clients that do not support sending and collecting (e.g. desktop) show only the card and state, and a tap guides to a capable client; an old version that receives an unrecognized payment card shows downgrade copy. Must define: what each client can do (view, claim, collect, return, initiate); the guidance copy; the downgrade copy (e.g. "[Transfer] Please upgrade to the latest version to view"); how the downgraded card appears in the chat list summary. | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: Medium (desktop has direct evidence from two products; old-version downgrade has direct evidence from two products)
- **[PAY-11] Fund state follows the wallet bill**: the chat card only maps fund state, and the authoritative record is the wallet bill; when a card is deleted, a record is lost or restored from backup, the balance and bill are unaffected. Must define: which prevails when the card and bill are inconsistent and the refresh timing; the placeholder copy when card details are missing; the entry from the card to the bill details; where pending funds are handled after a chat is deleted (see PAY-26). | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: Medium (direct evidence from two products)
- **[PAY-12] Red packet composition and expiry**: a red packet consists of an amount form (equal or random), a count, a distribution scope (one-on-one counterpart or group) and an expiry limit; after expiry the unclaimed portion is automatically returned. Must define: the forms selectable in one-on-one and in group; the expiry limit and its starting point (when the red packet is generated or when sent); the count and per-amount cap (see PAY-22); the lower and upper bound of each share in the random form (not below the minimum currency unit, not above the per-amount cap) and whether shares are generated at send time or claim time; whether a group red packet's count can exceed the group size; whether the sender can claim their own group red packet (mainstream allows it, and the count cap includes the sender; if not allowed, mark it as opposite to convention and put it into rulings); whether unclaimed funds return immediately when the group is dissolved (recommended: return immediately, do not leave funds waiting until expiry); the return destination (see PAY-21). | Magnitude: expiry limit 24 hours | Confidence: Medium (direct evidence from two products)
- **[PAY-13] Red packet states and claim details**: a red packet has at least four states, unclaimed, claimed, fully claimed and expired, the card changes with state, and the details page shows the claim records. Must define: both parties' card copy for each state (single share uses "Claimed", multiple shares use "Fully claimed"); whether a claim generates a system message in the chat (e.g. "{nickname} claimed your red packet") and whether the sender is notified; to whom the claim records are visible (see PAY-25); "Expired" and "Returned" kept separate, and the card and bill copy the sender sees while a return is processing and when a return fails (retry, transfer to support). | Magnitude: n/a | Confidence: Medium (direct evidence from two products, one of which is a client symbol name)
- **[PAY-14] Payment risk prompts**: See SAFE-14. Scope: in-place prompts when paying a non-friend, a first-time payee or a newly registered account, or when the other party asks for a transfer in the chat.
- **[PAY-15] Limit and rate-limit prompts**: See SAFE-13. Scope: wording for reaching the single-transaction or daily limit, lockout after consecutive payment password errors, and prompts for too-frequent collection reminders. For limit values see PAY-22.
- **[PAY-16] Payment password and verification code anti-fraud**: See SAFE-15. Scope: anti-fraud prompts on the payment password setup page, the payment verification page, and the system message where the other party requests opening a wallet.
- **[PAY-17] Withdrawal and large transfer-out cooldown**: See SAFE-30. Scope: withdrawal and large transfer-out thresholds after a new device login or right after two-step verification is enabled.

## Divergences (ask Box on must-ask; write default-OK per recommendation and list it)
- **[PAY-18] Whether a transfer needs the other party to confirm collection**: Option A: the other party must tap "Confirm collection", and an unconfirmed transfer is returned on timeout (funds in transit are frozen by the licensed institution) / Option B: instant arrival, no awaiting-collection state. Recommended: A. Rationale: recoverability; if sent to the wrong person and the other party has not confirmed, it can be returned. Background: A at two products, B at two. The feasibility precondition (whether the licensed institution can freeze in transit and return on timeout) is not something Box can decide, it is a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC: whether the licensed institution supports in-transit freezing and timeout return} into open items; ask Box only about the product trade-off of whether the other party confirmation is needed, and when the precondition does not hold only B is possible and Box is informed. | Level: Must-ask
- **[PAY-19] Awaiting-collection timeout duration** (when PAY-18 chooses A): Option A: 24 hours / Option B: several days. Recommended: A. Rationale: the longer funds are in transit, the more mis-transfers and disputes; the known value is 24 hours. The card must write a countdown note (such as "If not confirmed within {N} days, it will be returned to the other party"); when the licensed institution has its own limit on in-transit freeze duration, that prevails, and until it replies write {TBC}. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PAY-20] Whether the recipient can actively return**: Option A: while awaiting collection the recipient can "Return", returning by the original route after a second confirmation / Option B: not provided, can only wait for timeout. After collection no return entry is provided, and a separate transfer is the only way. Recommended: A. Rationale: recoverability, a mistaken recipient has a graceful way to decline; only one product provides it, while another instant-arrival product states it cannot be returned. | Level: Must-ask
- **[PAY-21] Return destination**: Option A: return by the original route (balance payment returns to balance, bank card payment returns to bank card) / Option B: uniformly return to wallet balance. Recommended: A. Rationale: predictable fund destination; one product is known to state the original route explicitly, and another has both statements coexisting. Which refund route is possible depends on the licensed institution, a source-of-truth gap; until it replies write {TBC: refund routes supported by the licensed institution} into open items, do not ask Box; when both are supported write A; when this requirement only supports wallet balance payment this question does not apply, write return to balance directly, and no longer put it into open items. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PAY-22] Limits and verification tiers**: Option A: tiered by verification level (basic verification low limit, enhanced verification high limit), with single-transaction, daily, per-red-packet amount and count, and group payment request headcount and times limited separately; when no verification is completed no limit is set, see WAL-01 / Option B: a uniform limit, no tiers. Recommended: A. Rationale: tying limits to verification is a standard requirement of licensed institutions. Write the tier structure per A; the values for each tier follow the licensed institution and launch-region regulatory requirements, a source-of-truth gap, so the card writes {TBC: single-transaction, daily and red packet limit values} into open items, do not ask Box, and the norms library gives no values; but each limit must be written in the card as a back-office configuration item (name, target, counting period and time zone, in-line copy when exceeded), so engineering implements by configuration and testing writes boundary cases by the configured values, and a bare placeholder alone is not allowed. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PAY-23] Age threshold and verification method for payment**: Option A: only require self-confirmation of being 18 or older / Option B: self-confirmation plus birth date check at the licensed institution's identity verification, and under-18 cannot open it / Option C: under-18 can use it with a low limit. The age determination basis and the default for accounts that have not completed age confirmation see AGE-07; this entry sets only the threshold at the payment entry; the threshold age and verification requirements follow the licensed institution and launch-region regulation, and until it replies write {TBC}. Recommended: B. Rationale: with self-confirmation alone, an account with an empty birthday can also reach the payment page, a high compliance risk. | Level: Must-ask
- **[PAY-24] Whether splitting and group payment requests use bookkeeping or real payment**: Option A: bookkeeping only, recording who paid, how it is split and who owes whom, with settlement done outside the app and members manually marking "Paid" / Option B: real in-app payment, the initiator collects from members, with funds flowing through the licensed institution / Option C: bookkeeping plus one-tap payment, the ledger records the split, members tap "Pay" to go through a transfer (regulated fund flow), and after payment succeeds the ledger automatically marks it paid. Recommended: C. Rationale: the ledger itself does not move money and has a low regulatory burden, and the payment step reuses the existing transfer capability. Background: of the five products only two do group payment requests inside chat, both real payment (B); independent splitting products mainly do bookkeeping (A), with some extending to one-tap payment (C); the recommendation is opposite to the in-chat practice of the two products (B), a regulatory burden trade-off. | Level: Must-ask | Framing: yes
- **[PAY-25] Who can see red packet claim records**: Option A: claimers can see all claimers and amounts / Option B: the sender sees all, claimers see only the list of claimers and their own amount / Option C: only the sender can see. Recommended: B, with no comparison markers such as "Best luck". Rationale: privacy, the amount is personal information; opposite to mainstream group red packets that publicly show claimed amounts and compare luck (one product's passphrase red packet lets claimers see others' amounts, and another product's group red packet opens claim details to claimers), a privacy trade-off. When A (the mainstream practice) is chosen, must define: who can see claim details (only members in the group at claim time or also members who joined later), whether "Best luck" is marked, whether amounts appear in chat system messages and notifications, how records appear after a claimer leaves the group or deletes their account. | Level: Must-ask
- **[PAY-26] Blocking on unprocessed funds before removing a contact, deleting a chat or leaving a group**: Option A: when there are pending awaiting-collection transfers or unclaimed red packets, a dialog states the count and offers "Go handle" and "Delete anyway"; after deletion the fund state is unchanged and can be handled in the wallet bill / Option B: no blocking. Recommended: A. Rationale: recoverability, avoiding users being unable to find the return entry after deleting a chat; only one product provides it. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PAY-27] Red packet forms**: Option A: one-on-one single equal share, in group two forms, equal and random / Option B: A plus designated recipients (designating several members in a group) / Option C: A plus passphrase red packets (claimed only by replying the specified passphrase). Recommended: A. Rationale: minimum scope for the first release; designated recipients are used by two products and passphrase by one, and can be added later. The random-amount form may touch gambling-type regulation in some launch regions (not confirmed), to be confirmed against launch region compliance, and until confirmed write {TBC}. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PAY-28] Collection reminders and nudges**: Option A: the initiator can "Remind them" on the awaiting-collection card or group payment request details, with a frequency limit (wording see SAFE-13) / Option B: not provided. Recommended: A. Rationale: user comprehension, giving the initiator an action to take before the awaiting-collection timeout. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PAY-29] Payment verification factor**: Option A: payment password or biometrics, verified on every transaction, not skippable / Option B: device lock or biometrics, skippable with a risk prompt / Option C: reuse the two-step verification password. Whether two-step verification must be enabled first see ACC-25. Recommended: follow the licensed institution and ZYMIX's existing rules; if undecided take A. Rationale: fund actions are irreversible and verification should not be skippable. | Level: Default-OK

## Must-ask states (module-specific, supplements the general state matrix)
- Feasibility precondition: whether ZYMIX already has wallet and payment qualifications and whether the licensed institution supports this feature; when unconfirmed write {TBC: what is missing}, merged with the other licensed-institution values of this requirement (limits, refund routes, whether in-transit freezing is possible, etc.) into one group of blocking open items placed at the top.
- Nature first: mark each state first as "regulated fund flow" or "bookkeeping". For regulated fund flow, the state follows the licensed institution's confirmation, and ZYMIX only displays it and must not rewrite success or failure on its own; bookkeeping can be edited or deleted by users, and deleting or changing it produces no fund change. When one card involves both, write two items.
- Regulated fund flow, submission: what the card and bill show for processing, licensed-institution confirmation timeout, callback delay and unknown result; whether repeated taps or repeated submission debit only once; where to see the result after leaving the page while processing.
- Regulated fund flow, blocking: insufficient balance, over the limit, risk control block, lockout after consecutive verification failures, account frozen, verification in progress or failed, region unavailable, each with its copy and recovery timing.
- Regulated fund flow, counterpart: not opened, not verified, region unavailable, account deleted, blocked me, I blocked them, each handled before and after initiation; how a pending collection is returned when the counterpart deletes their account (see WAL-18); what the payer sees when the counterpart wallet is restricted see WAL-22.
- Regulated fund flow, final states: collected, collected pending credit (whether it can be returned), returned by the other party, timeout return, red packet expiry return, each with both parties' card, bill and notification copy and arrival time.
- Bookkeeping, splitting: ledger creation, changing the amount or members, deletion, a member leaving or being removed from the group, manually marking paid and unmarking; marking paid does not mean funds have moved, and deleting a ledger does not affect payments that already happened.
- Bookkeeping and fund-flow linkage: when members settle through in-app payment, the ledger marks paid only after the fund flow is confirmed successful; when the fund flow fails or is returned the ledger stays unpaid; how already-paid amounts are handled when a group payment request is canceled by the initiator, reaches its deadline or has been partly paid (a fund return, treat as must-ask).
- Chat side: the chat holding a payment card is deleted, chat history is cleared, details are missing after backup restore, switching to a new device; where to find the fund state and the handling entry.
- Sender state: when the sender's account is deleted or banned, whether the unclaimed red packet can still be claimed, and the return destination and timing at expiry.
- Multi-device and version: what the card and available actions are on desktop or unsupported clients; when the card refreshes on another device after one device collects or returns; downgrade copy for old versions.
- Group: unclaimed red packets and unpaid group payment requests after a member leaves or is removed; group dissolution; a designated recipient leaving the group; what other members see when a transfer to a designated member is made in the group.
- Message linkage: what each shows when a payment card is quoted, reacted to, reported or unsent; whether a payment card sent in a timed-message chat disappears with the timer (the bill does not disappear).
- Compliance: account birthday empty or under 18, region change, identity verification information inconsistent with account profile, balance transfer-out after the feature goes offline in the region.

## Anti-patterns
| Generic/wrong | Correct | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "Supports transfers" | Write the intermediate states, both parties' view copy, the final state list, timeout and return destination, and blocking when the other party cannot collect | A transfer is a state machine, and missing one final state leaves the money's whereabouts unclear |
| "Transfer failed, please retry" | State whether it was debited, where the money was returned and how long until it arrives | Users care most about where the money is, and a generic failure copy causes duplicate payments and complaints |
| "Unsend transfer" | After a payment succeeds the initiator cannot cancel; write the route of the other party returning or the timeout return | Unsending a chat card is not recovering the funds, and mixing them makes users think the money was taken back |
| The card state judged successful by the client itself | Fund state follows the licensed institution's confirmation and the wallet bill, and the card only maps it | When local judgment differs from the actual funds, reconciliation is impossible |
| In a split ledger, "Paid" is taken as money received | Separate bookkeeping from regulated fund flow: manually marking paid moves no money, and only after in-app payment succeeds is it marked paid automatically | Mixing bookkeeping and fund flow confuses regulatory responsibility and the reconciliation basis |
| "Amount cap xx yuan" written as a fixed value | Limits are tiered by verification level and region, with values following the licensed institution and regulatory requirements | A limit is a compliance constraint and differs by region and verification level |
| A red packet described only as "send and grab red packets" | Write the forms, count, expiry limit and starting point, expiry return destination and claim record visibility | Expiry and return are the most common red packet complaints |
| Deleting a chat without mentioning unprocessed funds | Before deleting, prompt the count of unprocessed items and explain they are handled in the wallet bill after deletion | After deleting the chat users cannot find the return entry |

## Calibration sample
The feature easiest to write too generically: initiating a transfer in a one-on-one chat. After the tap there are four prerequisite checks: payment lock, network, whether the other party can collect, and limit, which form a decision chain; lock and network do not depend on the server response and go first; only after the limit passes does the payment password prompt appear for personal verification, and personal verification is not a prerequisite check but its own item (consistent with the withdrawal sample). The intermediate-state copy, fund destination on failure, desktop guidance and awaiting-collection timeout are written into the body per recommendation, with the values in the card illustrative; limit values, refund route and whether in-transit freezing is possible are all answered by the licensed institution and written as {TBC}, merged into one group of open items; whether the other party must confirm collection and whether the recipient can return fall under fund reversibility, and what the payer sees when the counterpart wallet is restricted falls under what the other party can perceive, all must-ask, so the card leaves placeholders.

Description:
1. Entry: tap "Transfer" in the one-on-one input bar attachment panel to enter the transfer page; after entering an amount greater than 0 the "Transfer" button lights up, and with an amount of 0 it is grayed out
2. On tap, check in order:
   a. 1-Payment lock check: whether this account is in the payment password lockout period
      i. Locked: dialog prompt: "Too many incorrect payment password attempts. Try again in {remaining time}", button "Got it", stay on the transfer page, keeping the entered amount
      i. Not locked: go to the next check
   a. 2-Network check: whether there is a network
      i. No network: toast prompt: "Network unavailable. Check your network and try again", no debit, keep the entered amount
      i. Has network: go to the next check
   a. 3-Counterpart state check: whether the other party can currently collect
      i. Wallet not opened: dialog prompt: "The other party has not opened a wallet and cannot collect for now", buttons "Remind them to open one" / "Cancel"
      i. Region cannot collect: dialog prompt: "Collection is not supported in the other party's region yet", button "Got it"
      i. Account deleted, blocked me, has not accepted my message request: dialog prompt: "Unable to transfer to the other party for now", button "Got it", the specific reason is not distinguished
      i. Counterpart wallet restricted or frozen: {TBD: what the payer sees when the counterpart wallet is restricted, recommended: the same copy as the previous line, without exposing the restriction}
      i. Can collect: go to the next check
   a. 4-Limit check: whether the amount exceeds the single-transaction limit or today's remaining limit, values {TBC: the licensed institution's single-transaction and daily limits and verification tier values}
      i. Exceeds the single-transaction limit: inline prompt: "You can transfer up to {amount} at a time", no entry into verification
      i. Within the single-transaction limit but exceeds today's remaining limit: inline prompt: "You can still transfer {amount} today", no entry into verification
      i. Neither exceeded: show the payment password input for personal verification
3. Personal verification: the payment password input can switch to biometrics; on verification failure inline prompt: "Incorrect payment password. {N} attempts left"; 5 or more failures within 24 hours: lock payment for 3 hours, close the verification dialog, dialog prompt: "Too many incorrect payment password attempts. Try again in 3 hours"; verification passes: submit the payment, the button shows a loading state
4. Payment failed: toast prompt: "Payment failed. You were not charged"; if no result is returned after 30 seconds of submission: the transfer page shows "Payment is processing. See your wallet bill for the result", the user may leave the page, and resubmitting the same payment is not allowed
5. Payment succeeded: a transfer card appears in the chat; whether the other party must confirm collection {TBD: whether a transfer needs the other party to confirm collection, recommended: confirmation required, return on timeout if unconfirmed}, feasibility {TBC: whether the licensed institution supports in-transit freezing and timeout return}; when confirmation is required the initiator's card shows "Waiting for {nickname} to collect" and the recipient's card shows "Waiting for you to collect"
6. Confirmation required and the other party has not collected within 24 hours: automatically returned, timing based on the server time of payment success, return destination {TBC: whether the licensed institution supports return by the original route or only to wallet balance}; after the return the initiator's card shows "Not collected by the other party, returned" and the recipient's card shows "Expired"
7. Recipient return: {TBD: whether the recipient can actively return while awaiting collection, recommended: provide "Return" with a second confirmation}
8. After a transfer is sent the initiator cannot cancel it, and long-pressing the transfer card does not show "Unsend for everyone" or "Forward"
9. On desktop only the transfer card and state are shown, and tapping the card: toast prompt: "View and collect on your phone"
Note: three items, whether the other party must confirm collection, whether the recipient can return, and what the payer sees when the counterpart wallet is restricted, must be decided before the placeholders are replaced; limits, return destination and whether in-transit freezing is possible are also answered by the licensed institution and count as one group of open items, to be replaced together once answered
