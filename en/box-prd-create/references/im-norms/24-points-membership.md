# Points, tasks and membership subscription

> Applies: read this file when the PRD involves points (earning, check-in, behavior tasks, expiry, ledger, points mall redemption, lottery-style mechanics), the boundary between points and cash, or membership subscription (activation channels, benefit display, paywall points, renewal and renewal failure, cancellation, downgrade on expiry, membership badge, handling during bans and restrictions). Points are a ZYMIX internal ledger, which is "bookkeeping" and not regulated funds; when tied to cash (cash actions awarding points, buying points with cash, points offsetting payment, redeeming for cash-type benefits), points themselves are still bookkeeping, what is regulated is the corresponding fund action, and its feasibility is written as {TBC} per PTS-13, checked against the "feature feasibility prerequisites" of modules 14 and 19. Invite rewards see module 20 (INV-), underage accounts see AGE-07, wallet balance see module 19 (WAL-), points and remaining membership period at account deletion see WAL-18 (combined with wallet balance into one question). Mainstream messaging products have almost no general points system, so most points-side entries are divergences, and most recommendations are derived by the conflict-ruling ladder. Evidence is in evidence/24-points-membership.md.

## Baseline (follow by default, high confidence)
- **[PTS-01] Show membership benefits before activation**: The membership activation page lists the benefits included before payment, and users proceed to payment only after seeing them. Must define: the benefit list compared with the free tier; price, billing period and auto-renewal explanation (clear display of price and period is also explicitly required by platform review rules); when benefits are adjusted by region or time, the activation page and the benefit description stay consistent; the prompt shown on the activation page to accounts that cannot activate (region not sold, age threshold see PTS-12). | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: High
- **[PTS-02] Explain in place when a paid feature is tapped**: When a free user taps a membership-only feature or hits a free limit, immediately explain that the feature or limit requires membership and what activation gives, and offer an activation entry; do not fail silently or show only "No permission". Must define: which features and limits are membership-only (whether features are tiered see PTS-11); prompt carrier and copy (name the specific benefits); the state of returning to the original action after dismissing the prompt; whether the original action continues automatically after successful activation; the counting basis for upgrade sources (limit reached, feature paywall, settings page entry, etc.). | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: High
- **[PTS-03] After auto-renewal is canceled, benefits remain until the end of the current period**: After a user cancels auto-renewal, benefits stay available within the already-paid current period and lapse only on expiry; canceling is not an immediate downgrade. Must define: the status and expiry date shown on the detail page after canceling (see PTS-05); cancellation confirmation copy (e.g., "You will not be charged after it expires"); whether canceling refunds the current period's fee (application store channels follow store rules, write {TBC}; self-operated channel see PTS-24). | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: High

## Conventions (default-OK, mark as suggested)
- **[PTS-04] Subscriptions are modified and canceled in the channel they were activated in**: A subscription is modified and canceled in whichever channel it was activated through (an application store or a self-operated channel). Must define: the in-app subscription page shows the activation channel and offers an entry and explanation for managing it in that channel; the prompt when the subscription page is opened on another platform or device; the prompt that uninstalling the app or signing out does not cancel the subscription (for reference: state it in the cancellation explanation); each channel's cancellation cutoff (e.g., how long before expiry) follows channel rules, write {TBC}. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence; the third has only generic subscription text on its store page and does not count)
- **[PTS-05] The subscription detail shows the current status and renewal date**: The subscription detail page shows the current subscription status, and the next renewal date while active. Must define: the definition and display of the other states (for reference: canceled shows the expiry date and renewal can be restored before expiry; grace period shows the charge failure and the grace deadline; expired shows the expiry date and a re-activation entry); dates shown in the account's time zone (basis see UX-36); the value when the renewal date falls on a day the month does not have (for reference: use the last day of the month); the sync time of the status across devices. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence)
- **[PTS-06] On renewal failure, notify and give a grace period**: When an auto-renewal charge fails, proactively tell the user, offer an entry to update the payment method or renew manually, and give a grace period during which membership benefits continue as normal. Must define: whether a grace period is enabled and how long (an application store channel's grace period is a developer-selectable setting in the store backend, and ZYMIX sets it within the tiers the store allows; a self-operated channel is likewise set by ZYMIX; not re-verified online); reminder carrier (banner at the top of the chat list, system chat or push) and copy; the downgrade when the grace period ends without renewal (see PTS-07). | Magnitude: days | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence)
- **[PTS-07] On expiry, fall back to the free tier**: After membership expires the account returns to the free tier; free capabilities are unaffected, and only membership-only capabilities and limits fall back. Must define: list where each benefit goes after expiry, item by item; existing content beyond the free limit see PTS-25; the membership badge disappears in sync. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence)
- **[PTS-08] An account has only one active membership**: An account can have only one active membership subscription at a time; when one is already active the activation page shows the current status, and the same membership cannot be bought twice. Must define: the effective time of upgrades and downgrades with multiple tiers (platform review rules require upgrades and downgrades not to produce duplicate subscriptions); the prompt when the same application store account has already subscribed for another ZYMIX account (follows store capability, write {TBC}); what an account already subscribed on another platform sees on this platform's activation page. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence, plus platform specifications)
- **[PTS-09] Invite rewards**: See INV-10 (whether to have invite rewards and who receives them); issuing conditions see INV-11, lock period see INV-16, invite records see INV-19. This module covers only the earning, use, expiry and redemption of points themselves.
- **[PTS-10] Issuing failure and cheating clawback for task and check-in rewards**: See INV-17 (issuing failures of task and check-in rewards are handled by the same basis); the clawback scope after a cheating determination see INV-18, which applies equally to task and check-in rewards.
- **[PTS-11] Whether specific features are tiered by membership**: See MEDIA-15 (file size and transfer); translation and AI see AI-22, emoji see RICH-52, ad removal see ADS-13, group size see GRP-16, pinned chat count see LIST-01, favorites limit see MISC-16. This module covers only membership activation, renewal, cancellation, expiry and paywall prompts, and does not re-decide whether each feature is tiered.
- **[PTS-12] Underage or age-unconfirmed accounts joining lotteries, redemption and membership activation**: See AGE-07 (determination basis and how to tighten); the age threshold for payment see PAY-23. This module does not set a separate tightening scope for underage accounts.

## Divergences (ask Box on must-ask; write default-OK per recommendation and list it)
- **[PTS-13] Whether points are tied to cash**: Option A: not tied: points are earned only through behaviors such as check-in, tasks and invites, and redeemed only in the points mall for physical goods or in-app benefits; they cannot offset payment amounts or be redeemed for cash or wallet balance / Option B1: one-way cash in: fund actions such as wallet account opening, top-up, currency exchange and spending earn points, and points still cannot flow out / Option B2: points can be bought directly with cash, and points still cannot flow out; on iOS, per the launch region's store rules, this generally must go through in-app purchase (see PTS-23), points obtained by purchase must not expire (see PTS-15), and whether this constitutes stored value is a source-of-truth gap / Option C: two-way: points can offset payment amounts or be redeemed for cash-type benefits such as phone credit, top-up vouchers and wallet balance. Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative; when undecided, recommend A. Rationale: points stay pure bookkeeping and do not enter regulated fund flows. Background: all known platform coins are one-way cash purchases (that is, B2) with personal balances not flowing out, and only creator income can be withdrawn after secondary verification; no product was seen offsetting payment with points. When invite and task rewards pay cash or wallet balance directly (INV-10 and INV-11 point to this entry), feasibility is the same as B1 and C. The feasibility of B1, B2 and C is a source-of-truth gap (whether awarding points for fund actions needs a licensed institution or regulator consent; whether buying points with cash, offsetting payment with points, or redeeming cash-type benefits constitutes stored value or a payment instrument); write {TBC} into open items, grouped with the other licensed-institution values in the must-ask states of modules 14 and 19; in any form, points themselves are still bookkeeping. When C is chosen, a refund of the original transaction returns the offset portion as points, not converted to cash (default-OK, write it this way); the refund path for the cash portion see PAY-21, which is a source-of-truth gap. | Level: Must-ask | framing: yes
- **[PTS-14] Whether points can be gifted or withdrawn**: Option A: points belong only to the user, and cannot be gifted to others or withdrawn; the statement goes in the footer of the points earning page and the redemption page, linking the points rules / Option B: points can be gifted between contacts. Recommended: A. Rationale: gifting lets points flow between accounts, making point farming and disguised trading hard to determine; the known platform coins' personal balances cannot be sold, withdrawn or transferred, with the statement in the footers of the purchase page and the spending page linking the terms. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PTS-15] Points validity period**: Option A: no expiry, handled only at account deletion or permanent ban (permanent ban see PTS-22) / Option B: rolling expiry by earning batch (yearly), earliest-expiring consumed first, with a reminder before expiry / Option C: all cleared on a fixed date each year. Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative; when undecided, recommend A. Rationale: user understanding, and no known platform coin has an expiry; if points can be bought directly with cash (PTS-13 chooses B2), platform review rules do not allow purchased points to expire. Points handling at account deletion see WAL-18. When B is chosen, must define: the number of days and carrier for the advance reminder, showing each batch's expiry date in the ledger, and the basis when expiry and redemption happen at the same time (the server-side deduction time is authoritative). An irreversible action. | Level: Must-ask
- **[PTS-16] Scoring scope and daily cap for behavior tasks**: Option A: social behaviors such as sending messages, posting feed posts, liking, commenting and following can earn points, each task type has a daily count cap, and only valid behaviors count (toward different targets, non-duplicate content) / Option B: social behaviors earn no points, and only one-time or once-daily behaviors such as completing the profile and check-in are rewarded / Option C: no cap. Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative; when undecided, recommend A. Rationale: keeps the incentive while the cap and valid-behavior determination stop farming. Must define: the valid-behavior determination basis (determination details are not disclosed to users); daily cap and day rollover in the account's time zone (basis see UX-36); the same behavior reported from multiple devices counts once; tasks must not make enabling system permissions such as notifications, location or the address book a completion condition (explicit in platform review rules). Background: no mainstream messaging product was seen awarding points for check-in or behavior tasks. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PTS-17] Check-in cycle and broken streaks**: Option A: consecutive check-ins earn rewards that grow day by day, restart from day 1 after a missed day, and start over after the cycle completes / Option B: a missed day can be made up by spending points / Option C: reset on a fixed calendar week, with no consecutiveness required. Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative; when undecided, recommend A. Rationale: user understanding; make-up check-ins turn points into a cost of maintaining a streak. Must define: day rollover in the account's time zone (basis see UX-36), determined by server time and not the device's local time; multiple devices on the same day count once; the reward ladder is published on the rules page; the prompt when tapping check-in with no network. Background: no mainstream messaging product was seen with check-in. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PTS-18] Points ledger**: Option A: the ledger records every change individually (earned, spent, expired, clawed back, refunded), with source, amount, time and status (pending unlock, credited, clawed back), and the balance page links to the rules and the ledger / Option B: show only the balance. Recommended: A. Rationale: users can understand their current state, and clawbacks and appeals must match the records; one known product's platform coin ledger and reputation score changes both show the source and add or subtract direction for each entry. Must define: ledger retention period; whether clawbacks and refunds link to the original record (for reference: link, consistent with the refund linking basis in WAL-03); the points ledger shows no currency units and is kept separate from the wallet ledger. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PTS-19] Confirmation before redemption and whether it can be canceled afterward**: Option A: before redemption a dialog states the item, the points required, the available points after redemption, and "Redemption cannot be canceled"; after a successful redemption it cannot be canceled, and points are refunded automatically if code issuance or shipping fails / Option B: cancelable within a time limit after redemption, with points refunded / Option C: redeem directly without confirmation. Recommended: A. Rationale: predictable and simple; the known platform coins' spending surface is a closed list, spending is never refunded, and refunds happen only when delivery fails per the agreement. Must define: per-person redemption limit; prompts for insufficient stock and delisting; whether pending-unlock points can be used for redemption (for reference: no); no duplicate deduction when the result is unknown; the deadline for filling in and changing physical delivery details; if the redeemed item is digital content or an in-app benefit, whether iOS must use in-app purchase follows the launch region's store rules and is a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC}. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PTS-20] What happens to points when the original transaction is refunded**: Applies only when PTS-13 chooses B or C. Option A: when the original transaction is refunded, claw back the points earned from that transaction; if the balance is insufficient, deduct down to 0 and do not record a negative, with the ledger linking the original transaction; benefits already redeemed with points are not recovered / Option B: after clawback the balance may go negative, and points cannot be used until it is made up / Option C: no clawback. Recommended: A. Rationale: corresponds one-to-one with the original transaction, and not recording negatives avoids collateral harm; in known platform coins, when a purchase is refunded the platform claws it back, and gifts already obtained are downgraded in sync and cannot be converted again. Where the funds themselves are refunded to see PAY-21, subject to the licensed institution's confirmation. Level rationale: A claws back only the points earned from this transaction and never records a negative balance, leaving other points untouched, so it is not within the scope of enforcement and is default-OK; the clawback in INV-18 reaches rewards already issued and involves cheating determination, so it is within the scope of enforcement and is must-ask; if B (negative allowed) is chosen for this entry, treat as must-ask. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PTS-21] Participation and rule disclosure for lottery-style mechanics (e.g., a wheel)**: Option A: a fixed number of free plays per day, with no way to spend points or pay for more plays; the prize list and the winning probability of each tier are published on the rules page / Option B: points can be spent for more plays / Option C: payment can buy more plays. Recommended: ZYMIX's existing rules are authoritative; when undecided, recommend A. Rationale: B and C exchange something of value for random prizes, and whether the region regulates this as gambling or promotional lotteries and whether platform review rules allow it are source-of-truth gaps, write {TBC}. Must define: copy for the no-win tier; prize issuing failure per PTS-10; whether underage accounts can participate per PTS-12. Background: mainstream messaging products show only channel-run giveaways (the prize being membership) and no wheel. | Level: Default-OK
- **[PTS-22] Points and remaining membership period during a permanent ban or restriction**: Points and remaining membership period at account deletion see WAL-18 (combined with wallet balance into one question); this entry covers only enforcement. Option A: on a permanent ban points are voided and the remaining membership period is not refunded; during a time-limited restriction points are frozen (cannot be earned or redeemed) while membership keeps counting down, and resume after lifting; the scope of wrongful-ban restoration see ENF-15 / Option B: after a permanent ban, points and remaining membership period are retained for a period (days), restorable if an appeal succeeds within it, and voided at expiry / Option C: points are not frozen during restriction and can be earned and redeemed as usual. Recommended: A. Rationale: consequences are clear when enforcement is final, with wrongful bans restored by ENF-15; freezing during restriction prevents a penalized account from continuing to farm or redeem points. Auto-renewal through an application store is not canceled by a ban, so the ban notice must say to cancel in the store (whether ZYMIX can cancel on the user's behalf is a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC}). Background: known platform coins are permanently confiscated on termination, with no compensation for membership benefits lost to violations. An irreversible action. | Level: Must-ask
- **[PTS-23] Which channels membership can be activated through**: Option A: only application store in-app purchase (the iOS and Android stores respectively) / Option B: application store in-app purchase plus a self-operated channel (wallet balance or bank card), with the self-operated channel open only on clients where platform rules allow. Recommended: A. Rationale: charging, renewal, refund and cancellation are handled by the store, and ZYMIX does not touch funds; the store rules of most launch regions require digital services to use in-app purchase in the app, while some regions allow external purchase links or alternative payments (e.g., iOS apps in the US store region may place external purchase links, other regions must follow store authorization, and Android likewise differs by region), per launch region and store rules, {TBC}; the self-operated channel in B is a regulated fund flow, and whether the licensed institution can do deductions and auto-renewal is a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC}. When B is chosen, cancellation and refund see PTS-24. | Level: Must-ask | framing: yes
- **[PTS-24] Cancellation and refund in the self-operated channel**: Applies only when PTS-23 chooses B; application store channel refunds are decided by the store, write {TBC}, and are not covered here. Recommended: A. Rationale: consistent basis across channels and easy for users to understand; two known products explicitly do not refund the remaining fee of the current period. - **[PTS-25] Existing content beyond the free limit after expiry**: Option A: the excess is kept but locked (read-only or hidden), restored on renewal, and not deleted / Option B: kept for a period (days to months) and deleted at expiry; a countdown reminder and a download or export are provided before deletion / Option C: the excess is deleted immediately on expiry. ## Must-ask states (module-specific, supplements the general state matrix)
- Nature first: label each state with its nature first. Earning, spending, expiry, clawback and refund of points are bookkeeping, and changes produce no fund movement; membership payment, renewal and refund are application store charges or regulated fund flows and follow the confirmation of the store or licensed institution, and ZYMIX only displays them and must not rewrite success or failure on its own. Feature feasibility prerequisites: points tied to cash (PTS-13), in-app purchase and external payment rules in each launch region (PTS-23), in-app purchase requirements for redeeming digital content (PTS-19), the statutory withdrawal period of the self-operated channel (PTS-24), regional regulation of lottery mechanics (PTS-21), the self-operated channel (PTS-23), statutory requirements for auto-renewal reminders (PTS-27); write unconfirmed ones as {TBC}, grouped with the other licensed-institution or regulatory values of this requirement into one blocking open item placed at the top.
- Earning: a task completed but its reward being issued or failing to issue (see PTS-10); continuing tasks after the daily cap is full; the same behavior reported repeatedly from multiple devices; day rollover boundary (e.g., check-in just before or after local midnight); check-in with no network; a behavior judged invalid earns no points; which version of the rules applies to tasks completed before a task goes offline or rules change.
- Redemption: insufficient available points; pending-unlock points; per-person limit reached; stock exhausted or item delisted after confirmation; result timed out and unknown; redemption succeeded but code issuance or shipping failed (points refunded); missing physical delivery details.
- Validity period (when PTS-15 chooses B or C): expiring-soon reminder; expiry and redemption at the same time; balance display after partial expiry.
- Membership activation: payment interrupted or result unknown; restore purchases (after changing devices or reinstalling); the same store account has already subscribed for another account; already subscribed on another platform; region not sold; underage or unconfirmed accounts (see PTS-12).
- Renewal, cancellation and expiry: inside the grace period and when it ends; re-activating after canceling but before expiry; excess content on the day of expiry (see PTS-25); the time at which benefits are reclaimed after the store approves a refund (follows the store notice, write {TBC}); before and after a price adjustment takes effect.
- Account: account deletion (see WAL-18); permanent ban and time-limited restriction periods (PTS-22); wrongful-ban restoration (see ENF-15). These three are written as cards in this draft (may be merged into one card), and not written as "fill in under the corresponding feature".
- Refund: refunded is an independent terminal state and is not merged into expired, because the prompt copy, the time at which benefits are reclaimed and the notification all differ.
- Multi-device: the sync time of points balance, check-in status and membership status across devices; when activation on one device takes effect on another.

## Anti-patterns
| Vague/wrong | Correct | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "Complete tasks to earn points" | Specify the daily cap, valid-behavior determination, once across multiple devices, and the day-rollover time zone | Without a cap and valid-behavior rules, it gets farmed on launch |
| "Enable notifications to get 50 points" | Tasks do not use granting a system permission as a completion condition | Violates platform review rules |
| Points rules say "can offset payment" or "can be redeemed for phone credit" without marking feasibility | Mark it as tied to cash, write feasibility as {TBC}, and group it with licensed-institution items | Points become stored value or a payment instrument, touching regulated funds |
| Points mixed with wallet balance on the same page and ledger, or points shown with a currency unit | Show them separately, and the points ledger shows no currency units | Users mistake points for money and the reconciliation basis gets confused |
| Insufficient points shows only "Redemption failed" | State how many points are short, that pending-unlock points do not count, and offer an entry to go earn them | Users do not know the reason or the way out |
| A paid feature, when tapped, shows only "No permission" | Explain that membership is needed and the specific benefits, and offer an activation entry | Users do not know how to unlock it |
| Downgrade immediately after canceling auto-renewal | Benefits remain until the end of the current period, and the detail page shows the expiry date | The paid period is taken back early |
| On membership expiry, content beyond the free limit is deleted directly | Lock and keep it, or a countdown reminder plus download and export | Unrecoverable data loss |
| The account deletion confirmation page does not mention voided points or store auto-renewal | List the voided items, and prompt to cancel auto-renewal in the store first (see WAL-18) | The user is still charged by the store after deletion |

## Calibration sample
The feature most prone to vague writing: points mall redemption. The three pre-checks, network, redemption limit and available points, form a decision chain; the pre-redemption confirmation, no cancellation after redemption, and automatic points refund on code failure are written into the body per the recommendations, with values in the card illustrative; this example has no must-ask items; the iOS in-app purchase requirement when redeeming digital content is a source-of-truth gap, so the card writes {TBC}.

Description:
1. Entry: the points mall item detail page; when the item is in stock the "Redeem now" button is enabled, and when stock is 0 the button is grayed out and shows "Fully redeemed"
2. On tap, check in order:
   a. 1-Network check: whether there is a network
      i. No network: toast: "No network connection. Check your network and try again", and stay on the item detail page
      i. Network available: proceed to the next check
   a. 2-Redemption limit check: whether the user's redemption count for this item has reached the per-person limit
      i. Reached: dialog: "Each person can redeem this item once. You have already redeemed it"
      i. Not reached: proceed to the next check
   a. 3-Points check: whether available points are ≥ the points required for the item; pending-unlock points do not count toward available points
      i. Insufficient: dialog: "Not enough available points. You need {N} more", buttons "Earn points" / "Got it"
      i. Sufficient: show the redemption confirmation dialog
3. Redemption confirmation dialog: shows the item name, the points required and the available points after redemption, with the prompt "Redemption cannot be canceled"; buttons "Confirm redemption" / "Cancel"
4. After tapping "Confirm redemption" the button shows a loading state, and the server deducts points and stock together
5. Stock has been exhausted: dialog: "This item is fully redeemed. No points were deducted", and the item detail page refreshes to "Fully redeemed"
6. Available points have changed (e.g., points were used on another device): dialog: "Not enough available points. You need {N} more. No points were deducted", buttons "Earn points" / "Got it"
7. Result times out without returning: toast: "Confirming your redemption. Check the redemption history for the result", points are not deducted again, and the redemption history updates once the result is determined
8. Redemption succeeds: go to the redemption success page, and the points ledger adds an entry "Redeemed {item name}", deducting {N} points
9. Code issuance or shipping fails: points are refunded automatically, the points ledger adds an entry "Redemption failed, refunded" linked to the original record, and a system notification: "Redemption of {item name} failed. {N} points have been refunded"
Note: when the redeemed item is digital content or an in-app benefit, {TBC: whether iOS must switch to in-app purchase}
