# Wallet account: account opening and verification, top-up and withdrawal, card binding, statements, collection and payment codes, freezing and closure

> Applies: read this file when a PRD involves the wallet itself: account opening and identity verification, top-up, withdrawal, card binding and unbinding, transaction details and statements, personal collection code and payment code, balance and freezing, and closing the wallet. In-chat transfers, red packets, collecting payment and bill splitting belong to `14-wallet-payments.md` and are not repeated in this module. Regulated fund handling for the wallet is usually carried out by a licensed institution; limits, fees, arrival time, verification materials and tiers, and the regions where it is open all depend on the licensed institution or regulators, are source-of-truth gaps, and are written as {TBC} into open items rather than asked of Box; multiple items answered by the same party count as one group of open items (e.g. "licensed institution: limits, fees, arrival time, withdrawal cooldown" counts as one group). Evidence is in evidence/19-wallet-account.md.

## Baseline (follow by default, high confidence)
- **[WAL-02] Top-up and withdrawal have a queryable processing status**: after a top-up or withdrawal is submitted there are at least three states, processing, succeeded and failed, and the user can find the current status in the transaction details. Must define: whether a status change sends a notification; the copy of each status in the details and on the result page; whether the user can leave the page while processing and where to see the result after leaving; the copy when the result is unknown (e.g. "Processing. See transaction details for the result"); the arrival time statement (depends on the licensed institution and the bank, a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC}); the notification destination (platform system chat is in BOT-09). | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: High
- **[WAL-03] Every fund movement leaves one entry in the details**: every top-up, withdrawal, collection and payment leaves one entry in the transaction details, with time, amount, type and status. Must define: the detail field list (transaction ID, counterparty, payment method, whether the post-transaction balance is shown); whether failures and refunds keep the original record (for reference: failures keep the original record marked failed, and refunds form a separate entry linked to the original transaction); the retention and queryable period of details (depends on the licensed institution's record-keeping requirements, write {TBC}); consistency with chat cards is in PAY-11. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: High

## Conventions (default-OK, mark as suggested)
- **[WAL-01] Fund capabilities require completed identity verification**: before enabling balance, top-up, withdrawal or collection, the identity verification required by the licensed institution must be completed; fund actions are blocked when no verification is completed, and there is no quota for unverified users (the tiers after verification are in PAY-22). Must define: the verification status list (unverified, verifying, verified, verification failed, additional information needed) and how each status is shown on the wallet page; where a block leads (straight to the verification page, or an explanation first and then entry); verification materials, tiers and review turnaround (depend on the licensed institution, a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC} into open items); how the verification result is notified; the age threshold is in PAY-23. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (direct evidence from two products, one of which replaces identity verification with binding the user's own bank account)
- **[WAL-04] Card binding and withdrawal only to accounts in the user's own name**: only bank cards or bank accounts in the user's own name can be bound, and withdrawals go only to accounts in the user's own name. Must define: the block copy when the name does not match the licensed institution's verification information; the supported card and account types (debit card, credit card, bank account, depends on the licensed institution, write {TBC}); the upper limit on the number of bound cards; the card binding verification method (SMS code, small-amount verification or bank redirect); card binding failures get copy by cause (wrong card number, reserved phone number mismatch, name mismatch, abnormal card status, bank not supported), and for bank-side causes direct the user to contact the card issuer; cards are shown in the UI only as issuing bank and last four digits; verification for unbinding is in PAY-29, and whether a card with in-flight transactions can be unbound. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (binding own card: direct evidence from two products; withdrawal to own account: two products, the second is indirect evidence: the withdrawal target is an external account linked to the user and may not have gone through real-name verification)
- **[WAL-05] A successful top-up cannot be revoked**: once a top-up succeeds it cannot be revoked. Must define: whether to prompt "cannot be revoked" before confirmation, and the copy; automatic reconciliation and return notification when the bank has debited but the balance is not credited (destination in WAL-10, timing write {TBC}); top-up sources and fees (depend on the licensed institution, write {TBC}). | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, the second is indirect evidence: what cannot be revoked is in-app currency bought for digital goods, not a stored-value top-up)
- **[WAL-06] Transaction details can be filtered by direction of income and expense**: details can be filtered at least by income and expense. Must define: whether to also filter by transaction type (top-up/withdrawal, transfer, red packet, collection code, refund); whether to group by month (grouping follows the account's time zone, see UX-36); whether search by amount or counterparty is provided and its time range; empty list and load-failure copy. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (direction of income and expense: direct evidence from two products; by type and by month: only one product, moved into must define)
- **[WAL-07] Disputes are raised per transaction, carrying the transaction ID**: when the user has a question about a transaction, they start an inquiry or appeal from that transaction's detail page, and the transaction ID is attached automatically. Must define: entry location and button copy; the list of issue types; whether a description and screenshots can be added; processing status (submitted, processing, answered) and result notification; the handling time limit (basis: see SUP-13, the value depends on the licensed institution and support capacity, write {TBC}); the boundary of what support can do is in PAY-03. The state machine: see SUP-10. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (direct evidence from two products)
- **[WAL-08] The wallet can be closed separately from account deletion**: closing the wallet does not affect the chat account or chat history; the way to delete the account: see ACC-21. Must define: the closure entry and confirmation copy; the scope of impact of closure (all bound cards released, handling of balance and details in WAL-18, WAL-20); the user's own verification before closure (see PAY-05); whether reopening after closure is a brand-new wallet; how an unsettled wallet is blocked when the account is deleted (see WAL-18). | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (direct evidence from two products)
- **[WAL-09] Own verification before fund actions**: See PAY-05. Scope: verification before withdrawal, card binding and unbinding, enabling the payment code, exporting details, and closing the wallet; verification factors and whether it can be skipped follow PAY-29.
- **[WAL-10] Where funds go when top-up and withdrawal fail**: See PAY-02. Scope: the destination copy for failed top-up, bank debited but not credited, and failed withdrawal (including a fee already deducted); the return path follows PAY-21.
- **[WAL-11] Limits and verification tiers**: See PAY-22. Scope: per-transaction and daily limits and verification tiers for top-up, withdrawal and collection-code receipts.
- **[WAL-12] Age threshold for account opening**: See PAY-23. Scope: account opening and payment use the same threshold; the age determination basis and the default for accounts that have not completed age confirmation: see AGE-07.
- **[WAL-13] Regional availability and balance transfer-out after a region goes offline**: See PAY-04. Scope: regional availability of the wallet entry, region changes, and balance transfer-out after a region goes offline.
- **[WAL-14] Unsupported clients**: See PAY-10. Scope: on desktop, account opening, card binding, withdrawal and payment code are not available; only balance and details are shown, and operations are directed to mobile.
- **[WAL-15] Withdrawal cooldown threshold**: See SAFE-30. Scope: the withdrawal threshold after signing in on a new device, just turning on two-step verification, or just binding a new card.
- **[WAL-16] Wording of prompts for limits, risk-control blocks and verification lockout**: See SAFE-13. Scope: top-up, withdrawal and collection reaching limits, risk-control blocks, and lockout after consecutive wrong payment passwords.
- **[WAL-17] Risk prompts for scan-to-pay and collection codes**: See SAFE-14. Scope: in-place prompts when paying a non-friend or a first-time counterparty by scan, or when a collection code is replaced or impersonated; distinguishing causes of scan failure is in DISC-06.

## Divergences (ask Box on must-ask; write default-OK per recommendation and list it)
- **[WAL-18] Asset handling on closure (wallet balance and unfinished amounts, points, remaining membership period)**: when deleting the account, these items are combined into one question to Box; when closing only the wallet, only the wallet part applies. Option A: the wallet balance must be zero, with no withdrawal in progress, no pending receipts and no open disputes before closure is allowed, prompting the user to withdraw or resolve first; points and remaining membership period do not block, and the closure confirmation page lists the points and remaining membership period that will be voided, which are voided when closure takes effect, no refund / Option B: closure is allowed, the wallet balance is withdrawn automatically to the user's bound account, and unfinished amounts run to their final state under the original rules; points and remaining membership period same as A / Option C: wallet same as A; closure is also not allowed while points or membership have not expired, and they must be used up or given up first. Recommended: A. Rationale: the user completes the fund destination personally, avoiding complaints about "where does the money go after closure"; points are bookkeeping and membership is a paid service period, so listing the consequences in advance is enough, and C would turn points into an obstacle to closure; one known product requires withdrawing first, and the practice of voiding balances for token-type products does not apply to electronic money; known platform coins are treated as forfeited when the account is deleted. Must define: when a cooling-off period is set (see ACC-21), points and remaining membership period are kept during the cooling-off period and restored when the closure is canceled (for reference); auto-renewal through an application store is not canceled by closure, and the confirmation page prompts the user to cancel in the store first (whether ZYMIX can cancel on their behalf is a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC}); how a balance below the minimum withdrawal amount or insufficient to cover the fee is cleared depends on the licensed institution, write {TBC}. Level rationale: it is an irreversible disposal (②); wallet, points and membership are combined into one question so that must-ask items for the account-deletion requirement do not exceed 4 (ACC-21, PRIV-17, this entry, and GRP-28 when the deleter is a group owner). Points and membership on ban are in PTS-22. | Level: Must-ask
- **[WAL-19] Whether details can be deleted**: Option A: no deletion provided / Option B: a single entry can be hidden from the user's own list, while export and the licensed institution's records are retained, and display can be restored in settings / Option C: a single entry can be deleted, not recoverable, and export still includes it. Recommended: A. Rationale: details are fund vouchers, and once deleted on the user side they no longer match the licensed institution's records, leaving no evidence in a dispute; it is the opposite of one existing product's practice (C) and is a data-integrity trade-off. | Level: Must-ask
- **[WAL-20] Details and vouchers before and after closure**: Option A: the closure confirmation page prompts the user to download details first and provides a download entry; after closure the user can apply for a transaction certificate through support / Option B: no details are provided after closure. Recommended: A. Rationale: recoverability; the licensed institution keeps records as required by regulation, which is not within the scope of deletion trade-offs, and the retention period is written as {TBC}. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-21] What is disclosed to the user on freezing and restriction**: Option A: the wallet page shows a persistent restricted-status bar stating the restricted capabilities (which of payment, collection and withdrawal) and the path to lifting the restriction; for the reason, give only the categories the licensed institution allows to be disclosed, the category list follows the licensed institution's reply, {TBC}, and until it replies write "Your account needs review" uniformly / Option B: write only "Account restricted" and provide a support entry / Option C: state the specific triggering reason. Recommended: A. Rationale: the user can understand the current status and knows the next step. The user must not be told freezing reasons related to suspicious transaction reports, law enforcement or regulatory requirements (tipping-off restrictions under anti-money-laundering law, per the licensed institution's compliance requirements, {TBC}); whether the balance can be withdrawn during freezing, and the materials and turnaround needed for lifting, likewise depend on the licensed institution and regulators, write {TBC}, merged with the category list into the licensed institution's group of open items. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-22] What the payer sees when my wallet is restricted**: Option A: the same copy as other states where the other party cannot receive payment (e.g. "Unable to pay the other party for now"), without exposing restriction or freezing / Option B: state explicitly "The other party's account is restricted". Recommended: A. Rationale: freezing is a sensitive state of the user, and the payer should not be able to perceive it. | Level: Must-ask
- **[WAL-23] Whether a collection-code receipt can be refunded in the app**: Option A: the collector can tap "Refund" on that transaction's detail page, fully or partially refunding the payer, with the return path per PAY-21 / Option B: no in-app refund is provided, and the two parties transfer separately. Recommended: A. Rationale: the refund is linked to the original transaction and reconciles consistently, and does not consume new collection or payment quota; it is the opposite of one existing product's practice (B) and is a recoverability trade-off. Whether the licensed institution supports refunds on collection-code transactions is a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC}; when unsupported, write per B without asking. | Level: Must-ask
- **[WAL-24] Whether a withdrawal can be canceled after submission**: Option A: cannot be canceled after submission, and failures are returned automatically / Option B: can be canceled while processing, and after cancellation the amount returns to the balance. Recommended: A. Rationale: simple states, with no race between cancellation and arrival. Whether the licensed institution supports canceling in flight is a source-of-truth gap, write {TBC}; when unsupported, write per A without asking. | Level: Must-ask
- **[WAL-25] Collection code form**: Option A: a fixed collection code where the payer enters the amount; the collector can additionally generate a code with an amount and a note, which can be saved to the photo library or sent to friends, and arrival triggers a reminder / Option B: only a fixed code. Recommended: A. Rationale: one known product provides it; an amount code reduces the payer's input errors. The per-transaction and daily caps for collection codes are in PAY-22. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-26] Payment code form**: Option A: the user's own verification before enabling; the code is valid for a short time and for a single use, can be refreshed and paused manually, is enabled on only one device, and must be re-enabled when switching devices / Option B: a long-term static code. Recommended: A. Rationale: a payment code that is photographed can be used for fraudulent charges, and a static code is too risky. The refresh cycle is set by business and engineering; small-amount verification-free payment is in PAY-29, and the verification-free amount value is written as {TBC}. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-27] Screenshot on the payment code page**: Option A: when a screenshot or screen recording is detected, prompt in place "The payment code is only for paying merchants. Do not screenshot it or send it to others", and refresh to invalidate the current code / Option B: block screenshots on the payment code page, with the protected scope in SAFE-08 / Option C: no handling. Recommended: A. Rationale: the system does not always allow blocking screenshots, and refresh-invalidation does not depend on system capability; invalidation needs server cooperation. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-28] Balance shown or hidden by default**: Option A: shown by default, can be hidden with one tap, hidden amounts display as asterisks, and the preference syncs across devices / Option B: hidden by default, shown on tap. Recommended: A. Rationale: balance is the most-viewed information in the wallet; it affects only the user's own UI and does not involve what others can see. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-29] Default payment method and failure fallback**: Option A: the user sets a preferred method, and when it fails the user is prompted to pick another, without automatically switching the source / Option B: automatically try the next method in the user's ranking. Recommended: A. Rationale: the debit source is predictable; it is the opposite of one existing product's practice (B) and is a trade-off on predictable fund destination. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-30] Details export**: Option A: export by time range, sent to the verified email after the user's own verification, with an upper limit on the span per export / Option B: download directly in the app. Recommended: A. Rationale: the export file contains full fund records, and sending it to a verified email avoids leaving it on a shared device. Whether to provide a certificate stamped by the licensed institution is written as {TBC}. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-31] Retry after verification failure**: Option A: show the failure reason category and remaining retries, and move to manual review once they are used up / Option B: only show that it failed, and route everything to support. Recommended: A. Rationale: the user can fix issues such as document photo quality or name format on their own. The reason categories and counts depend on the licensed institution, write {TBC}. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-32] Document expiry or additional information needed**: Option A: remind before expiry, restrict fund capabilities in stages after expiry, and the wallet page offers a supplement entry / Option B: restrict all fund capabilities immediately on expiry. Recommended: A. Rationale: gives the user time to handle it, and is recoverable. The conditions that trigger a request for additional information (e.g. a cumulative collection and payment amount threshold) and the grace period depend on the licensed institution and regulators, write {TBC}. | Level: Default-OK
- **[WAL-33] Fund movement notifications**: Option A: top-up, withdrawal, collection-code arrival, refund, restriction and lifting each send one message in the platform system chat plus a push / Option B: push only, with no chat record. Recommended: A. Rationale: there is still a record when a push is missed; the system chat form is in BOT-09. | Level: Default-OK

## Must-ask states (module-specific, supplementing the general state matrix)
- Regulated fund flow vs bookkeeping; feasibility precondition: first mark the nature of each state: balance, top-up, withdrawal, collection-code receipt, refund and freezing are regulated fund flows, subject to the licensed institution's confirmation, and ZYMIX only displays them and must not rewrite success or failure on its own; hiding details, notes, category labels and points are bookkeeping, and changes produce no fund change. Whether account opening, top-up, withdrawal, card binding, payment and collection codes, freezing and closure are each already supported by the licensed institution: write unconfirmed ones as {TBC: what is missing}, and merge them with the other licensed-institution values in this requirement (limits, fees, arrival time, etc.) into one group of blocking open items placed at the top.
- Account opening verification: unverified, verifying, verification failed, additional information needed, document expired; the same identity has already opened a wallet or exceeds the number of wallets per person; verification information inconsistent with account profile (name, birthday, region); birthday empty or under 18 (age determination basis in AGE-07).
- Top-up: bank debited but balance not credited; whether repeated taps debit only once; the source card is invalid or rejected by the bank; leaving the page while processing.
- Withdrawal: processing, failure return (including fee); withdrawing to a closed or reported-lost card; the query path when funds have not arrived beyond the stated arrival time; the wallet is frozen while a withdrawal is processing; balance insufficient to cover the fee.
- Card binding and unbinding: verification code timeout or error; the card is already bound to another account; card expired or frozen by the bank; unbinding a card that has in-flight transactions; withdrawal destination after unbinding the last card.
- Details: details inconsistent with chat cards (see PAY-11); the link between a refund and the original transaction; grouping basis across months and time zones; beyond the queryable period; ordering and refresh of in-progress transactions.
- Collection and payment codes: payment code shown offline and first reconnect; the original device's code after enabling on another device; restart after pause; screenshot, screen recording, screen casting; collection code replaced or impersonated by others; scanner is a non-friend or a first-time counterparty; collection code scanned when unavailable in the region.
- Freezing and restriction: what capabilities each of the three freeze types (risk control, identity information, judicial or regulatory) affects (the reason category shown to the user follows the licensed institution; judicial or regulatory reasons are not disclosed to the user, see WAL-21); in-progress withdrawals, pending receipts and the payment code during freezing; the scope of recovery and notification after lifting; initiating closure while frozen; what the payer sees at that time (see WAL-22).
- Closure: how each of non-zero balance, in-flight transactions or pending receipts, open disputes, and being frozen is blocked; listing and voiding points and remaining membership period on account deletion (the same question as WAL-18); the order of deleting the account versus closing the wallet; reopening after closure; behavior when the other party transfers to me after closure.
- Multi-device and versions: actions available on desktop and their guidance (see PAY-10); when the other device refreshes after one device completes verification or unbinding; fallback copy when an older version sees a new detail type.

## Anti-patterns
| Vague / wrong | Correct | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "Supports withdrawal" | State the three states processing, succeeded and failed, where funds go after failure, the fee and arrival statement (values TBC), and whether it can be canceled while processing | Withdrawal is a state machine, and omitting the failure branch leaves funds with an unknown destination |
| Hardcoding "withdrawal arrives T+1" or "fee 0.1%" | Write arrival time and fees as TBC, per the licensed institution's reply | These values are decided by the licensed institution and the bank, and hardcoding them makes a promise to users that cannot be kept |
| "Account frozen" with nothing after it | State which capabilities are restricted, the lifting path and entry, and how much of the reason can be disclosed | The user does not know what they can do and keeps contacting support |
| Showing the frozen status explicitly in the payer's dialog | The payer sees only the uniform "Unable to pay the other party for now" | Freezing is a sensitive state of the user |
| "Close wallet" with just one confirm button | State the blocking for balance and unfinished amounts, details download, the scope of closure impact, and the relationship with account deletion | Closure is irreversible, and where the balance goes is the biggest source of complaints |
| Details list only successful transactions | Failures, refunds and in-progress ones all keep records, and a refund links to the original transaction | When reconciling, a user who cannot find failed or refunded records will think money was lost |
| Payment code written as a statically shown "My QR code" | State enabling verification, short validity and single use, pause, screenshot handling, single device | A payment code that is photographed can be used for fraudulent charges |
| Card status and balance computed by the client | Balance and status follow the licensed institution's confirmation and the transaction details, and the client only displays | When local computation disagrees with actual funds, reconciliation is impossible |

## Calibration sample
The feature most likely to be written vaguely: balance withdrawal. After the tap there are five preconditions: payment lockout, network, wallet restriction, withdrawal cooldown, and amount and quota, so a decision chain is used; lockout and network do not depend on the server's return, so they come first; after the quota passes, the user's own verification happens only after tapping "Confirm" once more on the confirmation panel, and own verification is not a precondition, so it gets its own item. The three-state copy, the wording of the restriction prompt, and the display of the card's last four digits are written as body text per the recommendation, and values in the card are illustrative; cooldown, limits, fees and their refund, arrival time, and whether in-flight cancellation is possible are all answered by the licensed institution, written as {TBC}, and merged into one group of open items; whether a withdrawal can be canceled after submission concerns fund revocability and is must-ask.

Description:
1. Entry: tap "Withdraw" on the wallet page to enter the withdrawal page, where the destination card is shown as "{issuing bank} ({last 4 digits})"; after entering an amount greater than 0 and not exceeding the available balance, the "Confirm withdrawal" button lights up; when the amount is 0 or exceeds the available balance it is grayed out, and "Withdrawable balance {amount}" is shown below the input box
2. On tap, check in order:
   a. 1-Payment lockout check: whether this account is within a payment-password lockout period
      i. Locked: dialog prompt: "Too many incorrect payment passwords. Try again in {remaining time}", button "Got it", stay on the withdrawal page, keep the entered amount
      i. Not locked: go to the next check
   a. 2-Network check: whether there is a network now
      i. No network: toast prompt: "Network unavailable. Check your network and try again", keep the entered amount
      i. Network available: go to the next check
   a. 3-Wallet status check: whether the wallet is restricted
      i. Withdrawal restricted: dialog prompt: "Withdrawal is temporarily restricted on your wallet. View the restriction scope and how to lift it on the wallet page", buttons "View" / "Cancel"
      i. Not restricted: go to the next check
   a. 4-Cooldown check: whether it is within the withdrawal cooldown after a new-device sign-in or a newly bound card, cooldown duration {TBC: licensed institution's withdrawal cooldown rules}
      i. In cooldown: dialog prompt: "To protect your funds, please withdraw after {date and time}", button "Got it"
      i. Not in cooldown: go to the next check
   a. 5-Quota check: whether the amount exceeds the per-transaction limit or today's remaining quota, values {TBC: licensed institution's per-transaction and daily withdrawal limits and verification tiers}
      i. Exceeds the per-transaction limit: inline prompt: "Up to {amount} per withdrawal", does not proceed to verification
      i. Does not exceed the per-transaction limit but exceeds today's remaining quota: inline prompt: "You can still withdraw {amount} today", does not proceed to verification
      i. Neither exceeded: the confirmation panel pops up, showing the withdrawal amount, the fee {TBC: licensed institution's fee rules and deduction order} and the expected amount received, buttons "Confirm" / "Cancel"; tap "Confirm" to proceed to own verification, tap "Cancel" to close the panel and keep the entered amount
3. Own verification: the payment password input pops up, with the option to switch to biometrics; on verification failure, inline prompt: "Incorrect payment password. {N} attempts left"; ≥ 5 failures within 24 hours: payments are locked for 3 hours, the verification dialog closes, dialog prompt: "Too many incorrect payment passwords. Try again in 3 hours"; verification passes: submit the withdrawal, and the button shows a loading state
4. Submission succeeds: enter the result page, showing "Withdrawal processing" and the arrival statement {TBC: licensed institution's and bank's arrival time}; the transaction details add an entry "Withdrawal · Processing"; whether it can be canceled after submission {TBD: whether a withdrawal can be canceled after submission, recommended: cannot be canceled, returned automatically on failure}, feasibility {TBC: whether the licensed institution supports canceling a withdrawal in flight}
5. Still no result after more than 30 seconds from submission: the result page shows "Processing. See transaction details for the result", the user can leave the page, and the same withdrawal cannot be submitted again
6. Arrival succeeds: the details status changes to "Withdrawal succeeded", and the platform system chat receives "{amount} has been withdrawn to {issuing bank} ({last 4 digits})" plus a push
7. Withdrawal fails: the details status changes to "Withdrawal failed", the principal is returned to the wallet balance, a fee already deducted {TBC: licensed institution's fee refund rules}, and the platform system chat receives "Withdrawal failed. {amount} has been returned to your wallet balance", with "Contact the card issuer to confirm the card status" appended when the cause is on the bank side
8. The desktop wallet page does not show the "Withdraw" button and shows "Please withdraw on your phone"
Note: whether a withdrawal can be canceled after submission must be decided before the placeholder is replaced; cooldown, limits, fees and their refund, arrival time, and whether in-flight cancellation is possible are all answered by the licensed institution, count as one group of open items, and are replaced together once answered
