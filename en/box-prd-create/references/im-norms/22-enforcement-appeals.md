# Account and group enforcement and appeals (enforcement tiers, restriction notices, appeals, outcomes and restoration, reason disclosure)

> Applies: read this file when the PRD involves platform enforcement against an account or group (feature restriction, freeze, ban, group suspension), including restriction notices and lift time, blocking when a restricted feature is tapped, appeal entry and status, appeal outcome notifications, data during enforcement and after wrongful-ban restoration, and reason disclosure to the penalized party. For the account restriction overview see ACC-11; admin mute within a group see GRP-23, removal with re-entry barred see GRP-25; reports see module 09 (SAFE-09 to SAFE-12, SAFE-29); rate-limit notices see SAFE-13; wallet freeze see WAL-21, WAL-22; channel ban see FEED-13; copy for blocked sends see SEND-06. Hard regional requirements on enforcement notice content, appeal deadlines and human review are always written as "per launch-region compliance requirements"; this file gives no values for them. Evidence is in evidence/22-enforcement-appeals.md.

## Baseline (follow by default, high confidence)
- **[ENF-01] Enforcement tiers: feature restriction and whole-account ban are separate**: Platform enforcement has at least two tiers, "partial feature restriction" and "whole-account ban"; not every violation goes straight to a ban. Feature restriction first tightens outbound actions toward new targets (adding contacts, starting chats with non-contacts, creating groups or pulling people into groups). Must define: which tiers are used (warning, content action, feature restriction, read-only freeze, temporary ban, permanent ban) and, per tier, the list of restricted capabilities and the capabilities still available; whether the user can still communicate normally with existing contacts and joined groups during a feature restriction; whether a restriction also logs out other signed-in devices; the escalation condition for repeat violations; whether linked accounts of the same person are also actioned; the notice copy for permanent actions. Lift-time display see ENF-07. | Magnitude: a first feature restriction is mostly measured in days, lengthening for repeat violations up to permanent | Confidence: High
- **[ENF-02] The appeal entry is reachable directly from the enforcement notice**: The enforcement notice (freeze banner and detail page, ban page, blocking page on app open) provides the appeal entry directly. Must define: entry copy and position; where to start an appeal for a whole-account ban when the main interface is inaccessible (for reference: start from the ban page, direct evidence from two products); which enforcement actions are appealable see ENF-09, and how the page explains non-appealable ones; appeal form see ENF-10; whether appeals can only be started in the client or also through a web or support channel; whether review must involve a human and how long the appeal deadline is, per launch-region compliance requirements. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: High

## Conventions (default-OK, mark as suggested)
- **[ENF-03] Block in place and explain when a restricted feature is tapped**: During a feature restriction, tapping a restricted action (start a new chat, add a contact, create a group, pull people into a group) immediately shows that the feature is restricted and offers a detail entry; it does not fail silently or let the action appear to succeed. Must define: prompt copy and carrier per restricted action; whether the entry stays tappable (to explain) or is grayed out; whether the prompt carries the lift time (see ENF-07); copy and input-bar form for send-type restrictions see SEND-06, UI-D7. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence)
- **[ENF-04] Time-limited restrictions lift automatically on expiry**: A time-limited feature restriction is restored automatically on expiry, with no appeal or other action from the user. Must define: the criterion for lifting (the lift time in the server-side enforcement record is authoritative); whether the user is notified on lifting and through which carrier (notification carrier see ENF-13); state refresh on coming online after an expiry that happened while offline; whether a new violation during a restriction restarts the timer or escalates. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence)
- **[ENF-05] Platform action against a group is separate from action against member accounts**: A group suspended by the platform (paused or permanent) does not carry restrictions onto ordinary members' accounts; action against one member affects only that member. Must define: group-level action tiers (restrict some group features, pause group chat, permanent suspension, dissolve); under what conditions the group owner and admins are penalized along with the group (one approach: when they knew of the violation and did not stop it); whether the group suspension notice and the group owner's personal enforcement notice are sent separately; the appeal entry for the owner against a group suspension (see ENF-02); member view see ENF-17; channel ban see FEED-13. | Magnitude: N/A | Confidence: Medium (two products, direct evidence)
- **[ENF-18] Muted by an admin in a group**: See GRP-23 (a group admin's personal mute of a member; removal with re-entry barred see GRP-25). This module covers only platform enforcement against accounts and groups.
- **[ENF-19] Report entry and form**: See SAFE-09 (report target coverage see SAFE-11, report feedback see SAFE-12, follow-up actions after a report see SAFE-29).
- **[ENF-20] Wallet frozen or restricted**: See WAL-21 (what is disclosed to the user; payer view see WAL-22). When an account penalty carries over to the wallet, wallet-side reason disclosure follows WAL-21, not ENF-08.
- **[ENF-21] Short-term rate-limit hit**: See SAFE-13 (the throttling prompt for too-frequent operations; it is not an account penalty and does not enter the enforcement detail page or appeal flow).

## Divergences (ask Box on must-ask; write default-OK per recommendation and list it)
- **[ENF-06] Carrier for the restriction status notice**: Option A: a persistent banner at the top of the chat list that opens an enforcement detail page (type, restriction scope, features still available, lift time, reason, appeal) / Option B: no persistent notice, only a prompt when a restricted feature is tapped (blocking see ENF-03) / Option C: a blocking page on app open, entering the app after confirmation. Recommended: A; when a whole-account ban makes the main interface inaccessible, use C to carry the details and appeal. Rationale: users can understand their current state and check the term and appeal entry at any time; all three approaches are used by products. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ENF-07] Whether the lift time is shown**: Option A: both the detail page and the blocking prompt state the lift time (date and time, in the account's time zone, see UX-36), and permanent actions state "permanent" / Option B: only the detail page states it, and the blocking prompt says only "temporarily restricted" / Option C: no duration, only a qualitative description such as "short-term" or "temporary". Recommended: A. Rationale: users can understand their current state, reducing repeated probing and inquiries; this contradicts most products' practice (two products show the time, three give only a qualitative description or disclose nothing) and is a user-understanding-first trade-off. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ENF-08] How much reason and basis is disclosed to the penalized party**: Option A: only "violates platform rules", no specific reason / Option B: state the reason category (e.g., spam, fraud, harassment, impersonation), the rule clause relied on, the enforcement type and term, whether it was decided automatically by the system, and the available appeal and other remedies, without attaching the specific content actioned / Option C: on top of B, attach the specific content actioned (message, post or group name). None of the three options discloses the reporter's identity. Recommended: B, with the specific disclosure items completed per launch-region compliance requirements. Rationale: users can understand why they were penalized and appeal on that basis; this contradicts existing products' practice (all known products give only a "violated terms" level of explanation) and is a compliance-and-user-understanding-first trade-off. Level rationale: choosing C would show messages or group names sent by others to the penalized party, which falls under ①; the reason category for a wallet freeze is bound by licensed-institution compliance and follows their answer, leaving little for Box to trade off, so WAL-21 is default-OK; the two are assigned separately in ENF-20. | Level: Must-ask
- **[ENF-09] Which enforcement actions can be appealed**: Option A: all enforcement actions can be appealed, including time-limited feature restrictions / Option B: only bans and permanent actions can be appealed, time-limited restrictions wait for expiry / Option C: on top of A or B, some severe violations are final and not appealable. Recommended: A. Rationale: recoverable, and wrongly judged restrictions also get a remedy; some launch regions require an appeal channel for feature restriction, account suspension and termination alike. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ENF-10] Appeal form**: Option A: one-tap "Request review", with an optional appeal statement / Option B: a form to choose an appeal reason, write a statement, and optionally attach images / Option C: self-service lift after identity verification (SMS verification or contact-assisted verification). Recommended: A, with C also provided for freezes caused by account security anomalies (suspected theft). Rationale: lowest comprehension cost for the user; self-service lift applies only to security freezes that involve no violation judgment. State machine see SUP-10. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ENF-11] Appeal count and status**: Appeal statuses are four: under review, approved, rejected, expired; no duplicate submission while under review. Option A: each enforcement action can be appealed only once, and rejection is final / Option B: after a rejection, the user may add materials and appeal once more, and a second rejection is final / Option C: unlimited attempts with a cooldown between them. Recommended: B. Rationale: recoverable, a first decision upheld for lack of materials still gets one remedy, without letting appeals be spammed. State machine see SUP-10. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ENF-12] Whether an appeal handling time is promised**: Option A: after submission, state the expected handling time (e.g., "usually handled within {N} business days"; commitment basis see SUP-13: counted from server-side acceptance time, in business days, timer paused while the user is asked to supplement) / Option B: no time promised, only "Submitted. We will notify you after review." Recommended: A. Rationale: users can understand their current state, reducing duplicate submissions; one product gives a time commitment. The value depends on review operations capacity and is a source-of-truth gap; write {TBC} into open items. State machine see SUP-10. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ENF-13] Notification carrier for appeal results and expiry lifts**: Option A: send one message in the platform system chat with a push (system chat form see BOT-09), and update the status on the enforcement detail page in sync; on rejection, attach follow-up remedies (per launch-region compliance requirements) / Option B: only update the detail page status, no proactive notification / Option C: SMS or email. Recommended: A. Rationale: the result is something the user is waiting for; proactive notification leaves a record that can be revisited; when a whole-account ban prevents login, the result is shown on the ban page the next time the app is opened. State machine see SUP-10. | Level: Default-OK
- **[ENF-14] Data retention and the user's own access during enforcement and appeal**: Option A: during enforcement the user can sign in read-only (view history and export, but not send or operate) and data is fully retained; after the appeal period expires or the outcome is final, only the appeal entry is closed, and data is not deleted because of the enforcement / Option B: during enforcement the user cannot access the account, data is fully retained, and access is restored on unban / Option C: set an appeal deadline stated in the enforcement notice, and delete the account and all data if no appeal is made by then or the appeal is finally rejected. Appeal period length is per launch-region compliance requirements. Recommended: A. Rationale: privacy and data integrity come first; the user's own data must not become unreachable or be deleted because of an enforcement that is not yet conclusive; deletion goes only through account deletion, long-term inactivity and underage-account handling (see ACC-22, AGE-11). Points and remaining membership period during enforcement see PTS-22. | Level: Must-ask
- **[ENF-15] Data and relationships after wrongful-ban restoration**: Option A: full restoration; the account, contacts, group memberships and content removed because of the enforcement are all restored, messages sent by others during the enforcement are delivered within the offline retention period, and those beyond the retention period are explained in the restoration notice / Option B: restore the account and historical messages, but groups the user was removed from and content that was deleted because of the enforcement are not restored / Option C: restore only login capability, and the user rebuilds the rest. Recommended: A. Rationale: a wrongful ban is the platform's error, so data integrity and recoverability come first; groups already dissolved are not restored, and where a new owner has since been appointed, the restored user returns as an ordinary member. | Level: Must-ask
- **[ENF-16] How a penalized account appears to others**: Option A: a feature restriction is imperceptible to others; during a whole-account ban others see a neutral "This account is temporarily unavailable" with no mention of a violation; permanent ban and account deletion appear identical / Option B: restrictions and bans are both imperceptible to others, and messages still show as sent but are not actually delivered / Option C: explicitly state "This account has been banned for violations". Recommended: A. Rationale: enforcement is a sensitive state of the user and should not be exposed to others; B makes others' messages silently lost and breaks data integrity. | Level: Must-ask
- **[ENF-17] Members' chat and history after a group is suspended by the platform**: Option A: the chat is kept read-only with a top notice "This group has been suspended", history and group files remain viewable, and the suspension reason and group owner information are not shown / Option B: the chat is kept read-only and history is viewable, but group files and media can no longer be downloaded / Option C: the chat is removed, and the invite link and search behave the same as for a group that does not exist. Recommended: A. Rationale: members' history is not lost because the group was actioned, and users can understand why the group is unavailable; the suspension reason is told only to the group owner (disclosure level see ENF-08). | Level: Must-ask

## Must-ask states (module-specific, supplements the general state matrix)
- The moment enforcement takes effect: a message being sent, a call in progress, a draft being edited, a payment being processed (wallet side see WAL-21); when actioned while offline, what the user sees first after coming online.
- Multi-device: whether other signed-in devices sync when one device shows the restriction; sign-in on a new device during enforcement; whether a restriction also logs out linked devices.
- Group identity: management rights of the group when the penalized party is the owner or an admin; groups the user was removed from, dissolved, or transferred during enforcement; whether the user can return to the original group after restoration (see ENF-15).
- Stacked and changing enforcement: banned while already restricted; a penalty expiring and lifting automatically while an appeal is under review; an appeal submitted from two devices at once; account deleted during an appeal; effective time and multi-device refresh after an appeal is approved; appeal status transitions and notifications see SUP-10.
- Number recycling: the previous holder of a number newly registered by a new owner has an enforcement record; whether a banned account's number can register a new account or be rebound during the enforcement period.
- Others' view: what I see when I message, add as a contact, or pull into a group someone who is restricted or banned (presentation see ENF-16); the banned user is the owner of a group I am in.
- Group and channel suspension: a group suspended while a member is offline; a call in progress or live location sharing in the group; channel ban see FEED-13; service account ban see the "service account side anomalies" row in the module 13 must-ask states.
- Restoration: whether identity must be re-verified after a wrongful-ban restoration; messages undelivered during enforcement; how the enforcement detail page and the records in the system chat are displayed after restoration.
- Regional differences: enforcement notice fields, appeal deadlines, and whether review must involve a human are per launch-region compliance requirements; whether users in different regions see the same detail page fields.

## Anti-patterns
| Vague/wrong | Correct | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "Your account has been restricted" with nothing further | State the tier, restriction scope, features still available, lift time or permanent, reason, and appeal entry | Users do not know what they can do and only probe repeatedly and contact support |
| A restricted feature does nothing or appears to succeed when tapped | Block in place, explain the restriction, and offer a detail entry | A silent failure makes users think the other party is not replying or the network is down |
| An appeal is drawn as just a "Submit appeal" button | Specify appeal eligibility, duplicate submission, the under-review status, result notification, where to go after rejection, and the appeal deadline | An appeal is a stateful flow, and missing branches make it impossible to implement |
| "Clear data after a ban" | State what the user can access during enforcement, until when data is retained, and what is restored after a wrongful-ban restoration | Data cannot be recovered after a wrongful ban, so this is an irreversible action |
| A banned account shows "Banned for violations" to others | Use neutral copy, with permanent ban identical to account deletion | It exposes the user's sensitive enforcement state |
| After a group is suspended, the chat simply disappears from members' lists | Specify where members' chats go, whether history is viewable, and the owner's notification and appeal | Members' history is wiped out as collateral |
| The enforcement detail names the reporter or attaches another user's report text | State only the reason category and basis, and do not disclose the reporter | The reporter may face retaliation |
| An appeal result only changes the status without notifying | Notify in the system chat with a push, and sync the detail page | Users will not keep reopening the detail page to check the result |

## Calibration sample
The feature most prone to vague writing: the detail page for an account feature restriction and the request for review. After "Submit" is tapped there are only two pre-checks, "is there a network" and "does the server allow the appeal", so no decision chain is used. The notice carrier, lift-time display, appeal form (one-tap request for review, statement optional), appeal count and result notification are written into the body per the recommendations; values in the card are illustrative and real values are set by the business; the disclosure level of the restriction reason is must-ask, so the card leaves a placeholder; the appeal handling time depends on review operations capacity and is written as {TBC}.

Description:
1. Entry: once a feature restriction takes effect, a persistent banner appears at the top of the chat list, "Some features of your account are restricted. Tap to view details", and tapping it opens the restriction detail page; the banner disappears when the restriction is lifted
2. The detail page shows, line by line: enforcement type "Feature restriction"; restricted features "Add contacts" / "Start chats with non-contacts" / "Create group chats" / "Invite others to groups"; still available "Chat with existing contacts and joined groups"; lift time "Lifts automatically on {date} {time}", shown in the account's time zone
3. Restriction reason: {TBD: disclosure level of reason and basis, recommended: state the reason category, the rule clause relied on, whether it was decided automatically by the system, and the available remedies, without attaching specific content or disclosing the reporter}
4. Tapping a restricted feature during the restriction: dialog: "This feature is temporarily restricted and will be lifted automatically on {date} {time}", buttons "View details" / "Got it"
5. At the bottom of the detail page, "Request review"; tapping it opens the appeal page, where an appeal statement is optional (up to 500 characters), and the "Submit" button is always tappable
6. Tapping "Submit" with no network: toast: "No network connection. Check your network and try again", and the entered content is kept
7. Server returns that an appeal is already under review (including one submitted from another device): toast: "Your appeal is under review. Please do not submit again", and return to the detail page
8. Server returns that the appeal deadline has passed: dialog: "The appeal deadline has passed. You can no longer submit an appeal", button "Got it"
9. Server returns that the restriction has been lifted: toast: "The restriction has been lifted. No appeal is needed", and return to the detail page and refresh
10. Submission succeeds: the detail page status shows "Under review", toast: "Appeal submitted. Expected to be handled within {TBC: appeal handling time commitment}"
11. The review result is sent as one message in the platform system chat with a push; approved: "Your appeal was approved. Your account features have been restored", the restriction is lifted immediately and the banner disappears; rejected: "Your appeal was not approved. The restriction remains until {date} {time}", with "Learn about other remedies" at the bottom of the message, which opens an explanation page in the help center; the detail page offers one "Add materials and appeal again", and after a second rejection the appeal entry is no longer offered while "Learn about other remedies" remains
12. Automatic lift on expiry: the platform system chat sends "The restriction on your account features has been lifted", and no action is needed
Note: the disclosure level of the restriction reason in item 3 is a placeholder and must be finalized before writing
